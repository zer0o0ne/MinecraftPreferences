# RL losses
Status: approved (2026-11-05)
Stage: block 12 of `specs/00-overview.md` — the $H$-step preference advantage, clipped policy loss and factual-shift loss for R4, and METRA perception loss for R2

## Sources
- [U] the user, 2026-11-03: for each real state independently, compare it with each of the next $H$ real states by the MCTS preference value, discount the comparisons with the MCTS $\gamma$, and use their sum as the reward of the action chosen at that state. A state without all $H$ consequences has no PPO reward until a later sampling cycle supplies them. The reward itself is the advantage: no critic, returns, GAE or second discounting. PPO increases the probability of an action with positive advantage and decreases it for a negative advantage.
- [U] the user, 2026-11-03: use the stored MCTS visit distribution as the behaviour probability; recompute real latents and advantages with the current $\phi,P$ on every optimizer step; the PPO advantage and the factual shift target are stop-gradient. Gradients of the policy losses flow into $\phi$. The shift head is trained outside PPO proper, against factual consequences in the coordinates of the current $\phi$.
- [U] the user, 2026-11-05: starting `horizon` = 64 and `clip` = 0.2; no entropy bonus or KL penalty. `reward` receives `horizon` explicitly and accepts only complete eligible rows. R2/R4 keep the modules in train mode, including the policy's approved action dropout.

## Notation
- $\phi$ — current perception; $P$ — current preference network; $\pi_\theta$ — current policy with parameters $\theta$, including its input encoding by $\phi$. $D$ — latent size; $A$ — the action set of `specs/policy.md`; $m\ge1$ — number of per-action rows in a call; $i=0,\dots,m-1$ — row index.
- $t$ — a real action position of the endless timeline; $s_t$ — the real `StepRecord.before` state before the action; $a_t$ — the action chosen by MCTS at that position; $\mu_t(a_t)>0$ — its stored, fixed visit probability. The action and its input history follow the one-interval convention of `specs/policy.md`.
- $\lambda_t\in\mathbb R^{|A|}$ — the policy logits used for $\rho_t$; $\ell_t,\ell_{t,k}\ge1$ — history lengths; $L$ — `states` of `configs/datasets/perception-data.yaml`, the real-history cap; $R$ — the last policy readout used for its logits and shift.
- $z_t$ — the real latent recomputed by the current $\phi$ from the stored raw history; $Y_t$ — the latent history ending at $z_t$, truncated by $P$ and $\pi$ internally to their own configured history lengths; $C_t$ — the corresponding executed-action weights.
- $H$ — `horizon`, a positive integer; $\gamma$ — `discount` and $m_{min}$ — `min_p` of `configs/runtime/mcts.yaml`, not duplicate PPO hyperparameters. A position $t$ is PPO-eligible iff the continuous stored timeline contains all real states $t+1,\dots,t+H$. Eligibility is independent for every $t$; there is no bootstrap or partial-horizon reward.
- $k$ — consequence index, $1\le k\le H$; $(s_{t,k},u_{t,k})$ — the two logits returned by $P$; $p_{\succ,t,k},p_{inc,t,k}$ — their sigmoids; $V_{t,k}$ — the preference value; $A_t$ — the advantage.
- $\sigma$ — the logistic sigmoid; $\epsilon$ — `clip`, in $(0,1)$; $\mathrm{sg}$ — stop-gradient (`detach`); $\rho_t$ — the PPO ratio; $\ell^{ppo}_t$ — the per-action clipped policy loss.
- $\pi_\theta$'s action probabilities are the full softmax of `specs/policy.md` (5), with no action mask in the training forward.
- $\alpha\in\mathbb R^D_{>0}$ — perception's fixed metric weights; $d_\alpha(x)=\|\alpha\odot x\|_2$ — `latent_metric` of `specs/perception.md`; $\widehat{\Delta z}_t(a_t)$ — the current policy's predicted shift for the executed action; $\ell^\Delta_t$ — the per-transition factual-shift loss.
- $\delta_t=z_{t+1}-z_t$ — the current factual latent shift; $d_t=d_\alpha(\delta_t)$; $\zeta_t$ — detached unit METRA skill; $\varepsilon_{skill}$ = `skill_eps` > 0; $\varepsilon$ = `dual_slack` > 0; $q_t=\min(\varepsilon,1-d_t^2)$ — the clamped constraint penalty; $\lambda=\exp(\text{log\_lambda})$ with `log_lambda` a trainable scalar parameter initialised at $\log(\text{dual\_init})$, `dual_init` > 0; $\ell^M_t$ — the per-transition METRA loss.

## Math
(1) For an eligible $t$ and each $k=1,\dots,H$ [U], with the current $\phi,P$:
$(s_{t,k},u_{t,k})=P(\mathrm{sg}(Y_{t+k}),\mathrm{sg}(Y_t))$,
$p_{\succ,t,k}=\sigma(s_{t,k})$, $p_{inc,t,k}=\sigma(u_{t,k})$,
$V_{t,k}=(p_{inc,t,k}+m_{min})(2p_{\succ,t,k}-1)$.
The first argument is the consequence, the second its origin. Every pair is computed independently, not as a reused cumulative return. One `reward` invocation gathers all $mH$ pairs and groups them by `(consequence history length, origin history length)`; `P` is called once per nonempty group with the group's histories concatenated on the batch dimension, without padding. The resulting per-pair values are restored to their original row and $k$ positions, so $V_{t,k}$ and $A_t$ are 0-d tensors and `torch.stack(..., dim=0)` over $m$ origins returns shape $(m,)$.

(2) Advantage [U]:
$A_t=\mathrm{sg}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{H}\gamma^k V_{t,k}\right)$, summed in ascending $k$.
The immediate consequence has weight $\gamma$, as a depth-1 leaf in MCTS backup. There is no discounted sum over $A_{t+j}$, no GAE, no value baseline, no reward or advantage normalisation and no clipping of $V$ or $A$.

(3) PPO ratio and per-action clipped loss [U]:
$\rho_t(\theta)=\pi_\theta(a_t\mid Y_t,C_t)/\mathrm{sg}(\mu_t(a_t))$,
$\ell^{ppo}_t=-\min\left(\rho_t A_t,\operatorname{clip}(\rho_t,1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)A_t\right)$.
$\mu_t$ is fixed at sampling and detached in the loss. The current $Y_t,C_t$ are recomputed on every optimizer step; gradients reach the parameters of $\pi$ and, through $Y_t$, $\phi$. The computation of $A_t$ is under `torch.no_grad`, and `ppo_loss` also detaches its `advantages` input, so neither $P$ nor $\phi$ receives a PPO gradient through the advantage. The ratio is computed as $\exp(\log\mathrm{softmax}(\lambda_t)_{a_t})/\mu_t(a_t)$, a mathematically identical stable rewrite. The action mask is absent.

(4) Factual-shift loss for every real transition with $z_{t+1}$, whether or not $t$ is PPO-eligible [U]:
$\ell^\Delta_t=d_\alpha^2\left(\widehat{\Delta z}_t(a_t)-\mathrm{sg}(z_{t+1}-z_t)\right)$.
Both latents come from the current $\phi$. The gradient reaches $\pi$ through its shift output and $\phi$ through the prediction's input history, but not through the factual target. This term is the RL counterpart of the shift term of `specs/policy.md` (9), not a PPO term.

(5) Unit skill [U]:
$\zeta_t=\mathrm{sg}\left(\widehat{\Delta z}_t(a_t)/\max(d_\alpha(\widehat{\Delta z}_t(a_t)),\varepsilon_{skill})\right)$,
with the maximum taken row by row. A zero prediction stays zero. If $0<d_\alpha<\varepsilon_{skill}$, the result is $\widehat{\Delta z}_t/\varepsilon_{skill}$, not a unit vector. Both numerator and denominator are detached, so the result has `requires_grad=False`; METRA receives only the prediction's direction, never its magnitude or a gradient into $\pi$.

(6) METRA loss of every fresh factual transition [U]:
$d_t=d_\alpha(\delta_t)$, $q_t=\min(\varepsilon,1-d_t^2)$,
$\ell^M_t=-(\alpha\odot\delta_t)^\top(\alpha\odot\zeta_t)-\mathrm{sg}(\lambda)q_t+\log\lambda\,\mathrm{sg}(q_t)$.
The first sum is over the $D$ coordinates. Its implementation is `Tensor.sum` over the last axis; no sequential floating-point accumulation order is guaranteed. $\delta_t$ remains differentiable and $\zeta_t$ is detached. The gradient reaches $\phi$ through the inner product and constraint, and `log_lambda` only through the last term; it does not reach $\pi$ through the skill. At exact equality $1-d_t^2=\varepsilon$, the derivative of $q_t$ with respect to `actual` is zero and the derivative with respect to `log_lambda` is $\varepsilon$. There is no P1 pushing term, changed-mask, action-count bound or norm hinge in R2.

## Domain
- Inputs are real, finite observations and histories of one continuous timeline; all networks and latents share the caller's device and use the dtypes of their approved blocks. Each tensor function has $m\ge1$ rows. Exact tensor shapes, dtypes and action-index bounds are fixed in Interface.
- During R2/R4 the caller keeps $\phi,\pi,P$ in train mode, so the approved action dropout of `specs/policy.md` (2) is active. `reward` changes neither module modes nor `requires_grad`; only its local preference/advantage computation is under `torch.no_grad`. The policy and $\phi$ remain differentiable for (3), (4), and $\phi$ and METRA's dual remain differentiable for (6).
- The pending tail is not terminal and is never padded with zero advantages. Death/respawn handling is the environment's; no episode-boundary rule is introduced here. Trajectory/shard boundaries and perception-stream restarts do not interrupt PPO-eligibility.
- An action used for PPO has its originally stored $\mu_t(a_t)$, however many cycles later its horizon becomes complete. Sampling-time `StepRecord.delta`, `predicted`, `preference_close` and `preference_start` are not PPO, factual-shift or METRA training inputs.
- `alpha` is fixed and has `requires_grad=False`. `skill_eps`, `dual_slack` and `dual_init` are positive; `clip` is in $(0,1)$ and `horizon` positive. `MetraLoss` owns no mode-dependent behaviour.

## Interface
- `src/mcpref/training/rl/ppo/reward.py`: `def reward(P: Preference, origins: Sequence[Tensor], futures: Sequence[Sequence[Tensor]], horizon: int, discount: float, min_p: float) -> Tensor` — (1), (2). `len(origins) == len(futures) == m`; `horizon` = $H$ and `len(futures[t]) == horizon` for every row, so only eligible positions are accepted. `origins[t]` is $Y_t$ of shape $(1,\ell_t,D)$, `futures[t][k-1]` is $Y_{t+k}$ of shape $(1,\ell_{t,k},D)$, all $\ell\ge1$; lengths may differ. Every history is finite and in $P$'s floating dtype/device; `discount` is in $(0,1]$ and `min_p` > 0. Pairs are batched by their two history lengths as in (1), with one `P` call per nonempty group and one call total when all length pairs match. Row $t$ of the result is $A_t$: shape $(m,)$, dtype/device of $P$'s logits, `requires_grad=False`. The whole computation is under `torch.no_grad`, without padding or mode changes.
- `src/mcpref/training/rl/ppo/loss.py`: `def ppo_loss(logits: Tensor, actions: Tensor, behavior: Tensor, advantages: Tensor, clip: float) -> Tensor` — (3) per row. `logits` has shape $(m,|A|)$; `behavior` and `advantages` have shape $(m,)$ and the same floating dtype/device as `logits`; `actions` is int64 on that device, shape $(m,)$, with $0\le a_i<|A|$; `behavior[i]` = $\mu_t(a_t)>0$; `clip` = $\epsilon$. The function detaches `behavior` and `advantages` internally and returns shape $(m,)$ in `logits`' dtype.
- `src/mcpref/training/rl/ppo/loss.py`: `def shift_loss(predicted: Tensor, actual: Tensor, metric: Callable[[Tensor], Tensor]) -> Tensor` — (4) per row. `predicted` = $\widehat{\Delta z}_t(a_t)$ and `actual` = the current $z_{t+1}-z_t$ (not stored `StepRecord.delta`), both $(m,D)$ in one floating dtype/device; `metric` = $d_\alpha$, returning $(m,)$. The function detaches `actual` internally and returns `metric(predicted - actual.detach()).square()`, shape $(m,)$, in `predicted`'s dtype.
- `src/mcpref/training/rl/ppo/metra.py`: `def unit_skill(predicted: Tensor, alpha: Tensor, eps: float) -> Tensor` — (5), with `predicted` $(m,D)$, `alpha` $(D,)$ in the same floating dtype/device, and `eps` = $\varepsilon_{skill}$. Compute `torch.linalg.vector_norm(predicted * alpha, ord=2, dim=-1, keepdim=True)`, shape $(m,1)$; clamp it with `torch.clamp(norm, min=eps, max=None)`; divide the detached prediction by the detached denominator with `rounding_mode=None`. The result has shape $(m,D)$, `predicted`'s dtype/device and `requires_grad=False`.
- `src/mcpref/training/rl/ppo/metra.py`: `def metra_loss(actual: Tensor, skill: Tensor, alpha: Tensor, log_lambda: Tensor, slack: float) -> Tensor` — (6), with `actual` = $\delta$ and already normalised `skill` = $\zeta$, both $(m,D)$, `alpha` $(D,)$ and scalar 0-d `log_lambda` in their floating dtype/device, and `slack` = $\varepsilon$. Compute $q$ with `torch.clamp(1 - (actual * alpha).square().sum(dim=-1, keepdim=False, dtype=None), min=None, max=slack)`. The function detaches `skill`, $\lambda$ and the $q$ multiplier of `log_lambda` internally and returns $(m,)$ in `actual`'s dtype.
- `src/mcpref/training/rl/ppo/metra.py`: `class MetraLoss(torch.nn.Module)` — `__init__(self, cfg: PpoConfig)` creates exactly `torch.nn.Parameter(torch.tensor(math.log(cfg.dual_init)))`, a scalar float32 parameter. `forward(actual: Tensor, predicted: Tensor, alpha: Tensor) -> Tensor` applies (5) with `cfg.skill_eps`, then (6) with `cfg.dual_slack`, casting `log_lambda` to `actual`'s floating dtype; $\lambda=\exp(\text{log\_lambda})$ is detached. `groups() -> dict[str, list[torch.nn.Parameter]]` returns `{"metra_dual": [self.log_lambda]}`. Its inherited `state_dict()` carries the persistent dual across cycles through block 13.
- `src/mcpref/training/rl/ppo/config.py`: `@dataclass(frozen=True) class PpoConfig` with fields in order `horizon: int`, `clip: float`, `skill_eps: float`, `dual_init: float`, `dual_slack: float`; `def load_config(path: Path) -> PpoConfig`. Read `configs/training/rl/ppo.yaml` with `config_mapping`: missing/unknown keys and violated constraints raise `ValueError`; `horizon` is `positive_int`, `clip` is `number` strictly in $(0,1)$, and the three new fields are `number` > 0. Starting values: `horizon` = 64, `clip` = 0.2, `skill_eps` = 1.0e-8, `dual_init` = 30.0, `dual_slack` = 1.0e-3 [U].
- Block 13's policy wiring, with no additional PPO truncation or action mask: `R = pi.readout(Y, C)[:, -1]`, then `logits = pi.logits(R)` and `predicted = pi.shift(R, actions)`; `Preference` and `Policy` apply their own history limits internally. The same `predicted` feeds factual shift and, after (5), METRA.
- Block 13's history wiring: for every required real state $s_t$, obtain `RawHistory` by `read_history(out_dir, t, states)` from block 9. Split its global state indices at every multiple of $L$; encode each segment independently with the current $\phi$ through `Phi.frames_z`, using that segment's first raw line as its meta origin, concatenate the latents chronologically, construct executed-action rows from `RawHistory.actions`, and take the last $L$ latent rows. It obtains $a_t$ and the full stored probability vector through `load_step`/`StepRecord.action` and `StepRecord.probabilities`, and pending item references and eligibility from its own index/continuation state. Block 12 itself consumes only the tensors passed by block 13 and owns no pending dataset.
- Consumes: raw histories, actions and behaviour probabilities from block 9; pending positions and current $\phi,P,\pi$ from block 13; $\gamma,m_{min}$ from block 8; $\alpha,d_\alpha$ from block 2. Produces per-action/per-transition losses and `MetraLoss` for block 13. Datasets, checkpoints, files and progress bars of its own: none; block 13's checkpoints and `model.pt` hold `MetraLoss.state_dict()`.
