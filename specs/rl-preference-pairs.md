# RL preference pairs
Status: approved (2026-11-04)
Stage: new block 32 of `specs/00-overview.md` — R3 pair sampling, its persistent state history and the fixed preference dataset for one joint RL training cycle

## Sources
- [U] the user, 2026-11-03: pair sampling is a separate block between fresh R1 sampling and the first optimizer step of that RL cycle. Only seed states become vertices of its persistent History, and only History vertices may be compared or stored as comparison partners. A History vertex stores a raw game situation with the context needed by $\phi$, its insertion-time latent key, episode/time identity, usage statistics and averaged comparisons with other History vertices. Old latent keys are retrieval keys and are never re-encoded in bulk; raw situations are re-encoded whenever a new comparison or training requires it.
- [U] the user, 2026-11-03: seeds are sampled without replacement with probabilities proportional to the polarity of each state's comparison with its immediate predecessor, $\Delta$ ticks earlier: high incomparability or a large positive/negative preference change makes a seed more likely. These auxiliary adjacent-state comparisons serve only seed scoring and enter neither History nor the preference dataset.
- [U] the user, 2026-11-03: for every seed, sample temporal alternatives only among the episode's other seed vertices, with probabilities proportional to temporal distance. Select nearest alternatives from History outside the current episode by stale-latent distance, then expand them recursively through their most polar past comparisons in a beam search. Compare only seed–alternative History pairs, globally deduplicated within the cycle. Repeats across cycles contribute to arithmetic mean comparison results.
- [U] the user, 2026-11-03: only new comparisons of the current cycle enter cycle correction. Preference targets of retained arcs are their probabilistic predictions, not binary targets; incomparability targets remain binary. The incomparability threshold is lower for temporally close states of one episode and higher for distant states, with an additional sigmoid multiplier below 1. No temporal term modifies arc costs or the graph algorithm.
- [U] the user, 2026-11-03: after all comparisons, while History exceeds its configured capacity, remove the vertex with the smallest square root of its stored comparison count divided by one plus the number of unused cycles. Remove every reference to it and update the other vertices' counts. Empty nearest/beam results and an empty preference dataset are normal.
- [U] earlier decisions in this dialogue: selected references and corrected labels are fixed for the whole joint training loop. Training re-encodes their raw histories with the current $\phi$. A preference item contributes only preference loss, whose gradient reaches $P,\phi$.

## Notation
- $\tau_c$ — the fresh episode of cycle $c$: all recorded real states $0..T$, not only the states with executed actions. A state reference $x=(episode,index)$ identifies its raw situation and enough raw past to reconstruct every required history. Two references are equal iff their episode and index are equal.
- $\mathcal H$ — persistent History. Its **vertices are seed states only**, and every stored comparison partner is also a History vertex. A vertex holds its state reference, insertion-time latent key $z^{key}$, comparison records and usage statistics. Ordinary non-seed episode states never enter candidate sets, comparison records or $Q_c$.
- A comparison record on a vertex $u$ refers to another History vertex $v$ and holds $(\bar p_{u,v},\bar p_{inc,u,v},n_{u,v})$: arithmetic mean preference for the ordered pair $(u,v)$, arithmetic mean incomparability and the number of comparisons. The record on $v$ is its symmetric counterpart.
- $n_u=\sum_v n_{u,v}$ over the records currently attached to $u$; $a_u$ — the number of completed sampling cycles in which $u$ was not actually compared. Selection as a candidate without a comparison does not reset $a_u$.
- $N_s$ = `sample_states`, $N_t$ = `temporal_per_state`, $N_n$ = `nearest_per_state`, $B$ = `branch`, $W$ = `beam_width`, $D$ = `beam_depth`, $Q_{max}$ = `capacity`.
- $\tau_0$ = `base_threshold`, in $(0,1)$; $\lambda$ = `temporal_penalty`, $\ge0$; $\kappa$ = `threshold_scale`, in $(0,1)$.
- For a comparison $P(Y_u,Y_v)$ returning logits $(s,u_{inc})$: $p=\sigma(s)$, $q=\sigma(u_{inc})$ with `torch.sigmoid`. For scalar thresholds, $\sigma_0(x)=1/(1+\exp(-x))$, evaluated in Python float64 as `1 / (1 + math.exp(-x))`. For a current comparable comparison, $w=\max(p,1-p)$; the arc goes from the preferred state to the other one, with the seed first when $p=1/2$.
- $d_\alpha(x,y)=\|\alpha\odot(x-y)\|_2$ on insertion-time latent keys, even when those keys come from different versions of $\phi$.
- Every random draw uses the caller's `numpy.random.Generator`, checkpointed with the cycle. Stable-id ordering means lexicographic $(episode,index)$ ordering.

## Math
(1) Auxiliary seed scores [U]: for every episode state $t=1..T$, compute $P(Y_t,Y_{t-1})$ by the start-of-cycle $\phi,P$ in eval mode under `torch.no_grad`; with its $p_t,q_t$,
$score_t=\max\left(q_t,(1-q_t)|2p_t-1|\right)$.
For $t=0$, $score_0=0$. These comparisons are used only here: they produce no History record, corrected pair, label or training item.

(2) Seed sampling [U]: $n_s=\min(N_s,T+1)$. Let $U_+=\{t:score_t>0\}$ and $U_0=\{t:score_t=0\}$. First draw $n_+=\min(n_s,|U_+|)$ distinct states of $U_+$ without replacement with probabilities $score_t/\sum_{j\in U_+}score_j$, in the draw's returned order. If $n_+<n_s$, then draw $n_s-n_+$ distinct states of $U_0$ uniformly without replacement and append them in the draw's returned order. Thus an all-zero episode is a uniform draw, while insufficient positive scores never reduce $n_s$ and are filled from zero-score states. A zero-sized draw is skipped without an RNG call. Insert all seeds into History before choosing any alternatives; each starts with no comparison records, $n_u=0,a_u=0$ and $z^{key}$ computed from its raw history by the start-of-cycle $\phi$ in eval mode under `torch.no_grad`. An episode with no states produces no work.

(3) Temporal alternatives [U]: for seed $u$, every other seed $v$ of the fresh episode has weight $|t_u-t_v|$; sample $\min(N_t,n_s-1)$ distinct alternatives without replacement from
$Pr(v\mid u)=|t_u-t_v|/\sum_{x\ne u,\;x\;is\ a\ seed}|t_u-t_x|$.
Keep the draw's returned order as their discovery order. Only these other seed vertices may be temporal alternatives. With fewer than two seeds or a zero-sized draw this set is empty and no RNG call is made.

(4) Nearest alternatives [U]: among current History vertices outside the fresh episode, take the $\min(N_n,count)$ smallest $d_\alpha(z^{key}_u,z^{key}_v)$, ordered by $(distance,stable\ id)$. Historical keys are not recomputed. With no such vertices the set and its beam are empty.

(5) Polarity [U]: for a stored comparison between History vertices $x,y$,
$J(x,y)=\max\left(\bar p_{inc,x,y},(1-\bar p_{inc,x,y})|2\bar p_{x,y}-1|\right)$.
It is orientation-invariant and selects highly incomparable edges as well as comparable edges with strongly positive or negative preference.

(6) Beam [U], independently for each seed: initial frontier = its ordered nearest alternatives; visited = {seed} ∪ frontier; alternatives = frontier. Repeat at most $D$ times:
1. For each frontier vertex $x$ in frontier order, rank its comparison records by descending $J(x,y)$, ties by the partner's stable id, excluding the current episode and visited vertices; take at most $B$.
2. For every candidate $y$, retain the largest $J$ of an edge that discovered it at this depth; order candidates by descending that value, ties by stable id. Take at most $W$ as the new frontier.
3. Add the new frontier to visited and to this seed's alternatives. Stop early if it is empty.
Only selected frontier vertices join the alternatives; discarded branch candidates do not. Temporal alternatives are not beam inputs.

(7) Current comparisons [U]: for every seed, compare it with the union of its temporal, nearest and beam alternatives, all of which are History vertices. Globally deduplicate unordered vertex pairs in seed-processing order and alternative-discovery order; the first occurrence fixes the orientation (its seed first). Each unique pair is compared once in this cycle by the start-of-cycle $\phi,P$ in eval mode under `torch.no_grad`. The result is added to History before correction:
$n_{u,v}\leftarrow n_{u,v}+1$,
$\bar p_{u,v}\leftarrow\bar p_{u,v}+(p-\bar p_{u,v})/n_{u,v}$,
$\bar p_{inc,u,v}\leftarrow\bar p_{inc,u,v}+(q-\bar p_{inc,u,v})/n_{u,v}$;
the reverse record uses $1-\bar p_{u,v}$ and the same $\bar p_{inc},n$. A vertex participating in at least one current comparison has $a_u=0$; every other History vertex has $a_u\leftarrow a_u+1$ at the end of this cycle.

(8) Temporal threshold [U]: for two vertices of one episode with $d=|t_u-t_v|\ge1$,
$\theta(d)=\kappa\,\sigma_0\left(\operatorname{logit}(\tau_0)-\lambda/d\right)$.
For vertices of different episodes, $\theta=\kappa\tau_0$. The pair is initially incomparable iff $q>\theta$; otherwise it is comparable. Thus close pairs have a lower incomparability threshold, distant pairs approach $\kappa\tau_0$, and the multiplier keeps every threshold below $\kappa<1$. This rule changes only the verdict, not $p,q$ or the graph arc costs.

(9) Correction [U]: build one directed graph from only the current cycle's unique comparisons. Every comparable pair contributes one arc from the preferred state with cost $w-1/2$; initially incomparable pairs contribute no arc. Apply `removed_arcs` of `specs/acyclic.md` (2), (3), preserving its vertex/arc ordering rules. A retained arc $(u,v)$ produces pair $(u,v)$ with labels `{"p": w, "inc": 0}` — the original probabilistic prediction $w$, not 1. A removed arc likewise produces its preferred-first pair $(u,v)$, with `{"p": null, "inc": 1}` as in `specs/acyclic.md` (4). An initially incomparable pair produces its original comparison pair with `{"p": null, "inc": 1}`. Historical means and old corrected datasets are not inputs to this correction.

(10) Cleanup [U], after (7)–(9): while the number of History vertices exceeds $Q_{max}$, compute for every vertex
$score_u=\sqrt{n_u/(a_u+1)}$.
Remove a vertex with the smallest score, breaking ties by larger $a_u$, then stable id. Delete every record referring to it from the other vertices and subtract each deleted record's count from those vertices' $n$; their $a$ is unchanged. Recompute affected scores and repeat until the size is at most $Q_{max}$.

(11) Freezing and training [U]: the corrected pairs and labels of (9) are the cycle's fixed preference dataset $Q_c$. Their raw state references never change during joint training; their histories are re-encoded by the then-current $\phi$ on every use. Historical latent keys, historical means and old labels are not training inputs. Preference items cause only preference loss, with gradients into $P,\phi$.

## Domain
- $\phi,P$ used for auxiliary scores, key computation and current comparisons are the start-of-cycle versions, in eval mode under `torch.no_grad`; the caller's module modes are restored afterwards. Raw histories are finite and on the caller's device. All three operations use the same two network versions even though History keys originate in older networks.
- $N_s,N_t,N_n,B,W,D$ are non-negative integers and $Q_{max}$ a positive integer. Zero values disable their corresponding candidate source or beam expansion. Empty History, empty candidate sets and empty $Q_c$ are valid. A one-seed episode has no temporal alternatives but may still have nearest/beam alternatives from older History.
- Current-cycle deduplication treats $(u,v)$ and $(v,u)$ as one pair. Repeated comparisons in later cycles are allowed and averaged. Correction has no `dropped` verdict.

## Interface
- `src/mcpref/rl_pairs/types.py`: `@dataclass(frozen=True, order=True) class StateRef` (`episode: str`, `index: int`), the stable id and external raw-state reference; `Encoder = Callable[[Sequence[StateRef]], Sequence[Tensor]]`, supplied by block 9/13 and returning one $Y$ of batch size 1 per reference, in input order. `Encoder` performs evaluation-mode encoding and restores its networks' modes; all its calls in this block are under `torch.no_grad`. The caller guarantees that the referenced raw episodes remain readable for every stored History generation and every $Q_c$ that refers to them. `@dataclass(frozen=True) class CurrentComparison` (`a: StateRef`, `b: StateRef`, `p: float`, `inc: float`) — the ordered raw result needed by (7)–(9), retained independently of the updated means.
- `src/mcpref/rl_pairs/config.py`: `@dataclass(frozen=True) class RLPreferencePairsConfig`; `def load_config(path: Path) -> RLPreferencePairsConfig` using `config_mapping` and `relative`, rejecting missing/unknown keys and violated constraints with `ValueError`.
- `src/mcpref/rl_pairs/history.py`: storage-backed `History` and frozen vertex/comparison records. Public operations: load the latest complete generation; `get(ref: StateRef) -> Vertex | None`; iterate vertices outside an episode one shard at a time; stage seed insertion and symmetric mean updates; age unused vertices; perform (10); publish a new generation. No operation materialises all vertices, keys or raw states in memory.
- `src/mcpref/rl_pairs/sample.py`: `def seed_scores(states: Sequence[StateRef], encode: Encoder, P: Preference, scores_dir: Path) -> Path` — (1), writing score shards and returning their directory; `def choose_seeds(scores_dir: Path, count: int, rng: np.random.Generator) -> list[int]` — (2)'s draws, reading score shards and returning only the selected indices; `def temporal(seeds: Sequence[int], seed: int, count: int, rng: np.random.Generator) -> list[int]` — (3); `def nearest(seed: StateRef, history: History, alpha: Tensor, count: int) -> list[StateRef]` — (4); `def beam(seed: StateRef, frontier: Sequence[StateRef], history: History, cfg: RLPreferencePairsConfig) -> list[StateRef]` — (5), (6); `def select(states: Sequence[StateRef], history: History, encode: Encoder, P: Preference, alpha: Tensor, cfg: RLPreferencePairsConfig, rng: np.random.Generator, work_dir: Path) -> Path` — (1)–(7), writing per-seed raw-work shards and returning their directory.
- `src/mcpref/rl_pairs/label.py`: `def threshold(a: StateRef, b: StateRef, cfg: RLPreferencePairsConfig) -> float` — (8); `def correct(work_dir: Path, out_dir: Path, cfg: RLPreferencePairsConfig) -> int` — (9), returning the corrected pair count and writing $Q_c$ shards rather than returning them. Arc costs are `Fraction(str(w)) - Fraction(1, 2)` in current-comparison order; vertex ids are first appearance over the arcs, tail before head. Removed and retained arcs keep preferred-first orientation.
- `src/mcpref/rl_pairs/cycle.py`: `def sample_pairs(cfg: RLPreferencePairsConfig, cycle: int, states: Sequence[StateRef], encode: Encoder, P: Preference, alpha: Tensor, rng: np.random.Generator) -> Path` — one complete cycle, its resumable work files and the path to $Q_c$. It restores the caller's `P.training` after eval/`no_grad` use.
- `configs/rl-preference-pairs.yaml`: fields in order `history_dir`, `out_dir` (non-empty relative paths without `..`); `sample_states`, `temporal_per_state`, `nearest_per_state`, `branch`, `beam_width`, `beam_depth` (integers ≥0); `capacity`, `items_per_shard`, `history_per_shard` (`positive_int`); `base_threshold` (`number`, in $(0,1)$); `temporal_penalty` (`number`, ≥0); `threshold_scale` (`number`, in $(0,1)$). The RNG is supplied and checkpointed by the caller, so there is no independent seed field.
- Starting values: `history_dir` = `data/rl_preference_history`, `out_dir` = `data/rl_preference_pairs`, `sample_states` = 32, `temporal_per_state` = 4, `nearest_per_state` = 4, `branch` = 4, `beam_width` = 6, `beam_depth` = 3, `capacity` = 10000, `items_per_shard` = 256, `history_per_shard` = 256, `base_threshold` = 0.5, `temporal_penalty` = 1.0, `threshold_scale` = 0.95.
- Consumes: the episode's ordered `StateRef`s and `Encoder` from block 9/13; start-of-cycle $P$ and $\alpha$; `removed_arcs` from block 3; the previous persistent History. Raw observations are never copied into this block's JSON.
- Produces for block 13: `<out_dir>/<cycle:06d>/run.json` and `shards/<name>/items.json`, each item `{a: StateRef as {episode,index}, b: likewise, labels: {p, inc}}` of (9). Produces `<history_dir>/<cycle:06d>/run.json` and `shards/<name>/vertices.json` for the next cycle.
- History shard format: vertices sorted by stable id, at most `history_per_shard` per shard; each entry is `{episode,index,key,unused,comparisons}`, where `key` is the insertion-time latent as JSON numbers and each comparison is `{episode,index,p,inc,n}` with the oriented arithmetic means and count of Notation. $n_u$ is derived from these records. Only the newest complete History generation is retained; an older generation is deleted only after the new one is complete.
- Cycle work and rerun: `<out_dir>/<cycle:06d>/scores/<name>/scores.json` stores auxiliary scores in episode order, at most `items_per_shard` per shard. `<out_dir>/<cycle:06d>/work/<seed ordinal:06d>/result.json` stores the seed, ordered alternatives, all its raw `CurrentComparison`s and the RNG state after that seed; seed ordinals are processing positions $0..n_s-1$, not episode indices. `<out_dir>/<cycle:06d>/run.json` records the config, cycle, input episode identity, completed phase and RNG state. Every file/shard is written atomically. A rerun keeps complete score, seed-work and $Q_c$ shards and the newest complete History generation and continues after the last completed phase.
- History commit order: all raw seed-work and $Q_c$ are complete before any new History generation is published. Staged seed insertions, mean updates, aging and cleanup are not treated as committed. After an interruption, an incomplete new generation is discarded and deterministically reconstructed from the old complete generation plus raw seed-work; completed network comparisons are never repeated and no persisted delta is applied twice. A complete new generation then replaces the old one. This makes every crash boundary exactly-once without replaying network calls.
- $Q_c$ is an immutable snapshot: later History eviction cannot invalidate its external `StateRef`s or change its items. History, score, raw-work and $Q_c$ readers load one shard at a time. Correction stores its current-cycle graph as one `graph.json` work shard; that single graph work item, the current raw-work shard and continuation state may be resident, never the whole raw comparison dataset or all of $Q_c$. Datasets have config/input fingerprints and completion markers; an existing complete $Q_c$ and History generation makes the cycle `already done`.
- Progress bars: `rl seed scoring` (unit `state`), total $T$, restored initial the complete scored states, advanced after each auxiliary comparison; `rl pair sampling` (unit `seed`), total $n_s$, restored initial the complete seed-work shards, advanced after each seed; `rl pair correction` (unit `pair`), total the unique current comparisons, restored initial the corrected items, advanced per item; `rl pair cleanup` (unit `vertex`), total the initial capacity excess, restored initial the removals already published, advanced per removal. Each has the appropriate `<desc>: nothing to do` for zero remaining work because the input is empty and `<desc>: already done` when an earlier run completed it.

## Tests
- Auxiliary scores against sigmoid/polarity oracles: immediate predecessor only, $t=0$, incomparable and strong positive/negative cases, no History or $Q_c$ side effects. Seeded weighted positive-score draws without replacement, uniform zero-score filling when positives are insufficient, the all-zero case, skipped zero-sized RNG calls and deterministic processing order against the numpy branch in [N].
- Only seeds become vertices or comparison partners; temporal probabilities, counts and returned order among current seeds; stale keys remain unchanged; nearest distances and stable ties are hand-computed.
- Polarity's three extreme cases and neutral case; beam branching, global width, depth, deduplication, visited/current-episode exclusion, best-discovery-$J$ and early stop.
- Current-pair global deduplication and orientation; arithmetic means in both orientations, repeat counts, usage aging only after actual comparisons and no-gradient/current-network calls.
- Thresholds by hand: close/distant/cross-episode, $\lambda=0$, the $\kappa$ cap, strict `>`; verdicts leave $p,q$ and arc costs unchanged. Arc `Fraction` conversion and vertex/arc order are fixed.
- Correction against `removed_arcs` and independent labels: retained soft $w$, preferred-first orientation of retained and removed arcs, removed/initially incomparable binary inc with null p, current-cycle-only graph and unchanged old data.
- Cleanup scores, ties, repeated removal, symmetric reference deletion and count subtraction; exact capacity boundary.
- Fixed raw references/labels under later network changes and eviction; preference-only training semantics. Interruptions after a score shard, seed-work shard, correction shard and History generation: byte-identical continuation with no repeated side effects; $Q_c$ readable after eviction; config loader, fingerprints, completion and all progress lines/initial positions.
