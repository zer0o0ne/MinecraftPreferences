## Notation
- Paths: `index_dir`, `out_dir`, the directories of `{X}/run.json` and the clip paths of the items are resolved against the repository root `mcpref.REPO` wherever read or written; messages show them as written.
- $X$ — `index_dir`, the `out_dir` of train-index, as written; from `{X}/run.json`: $L$ = `states`, $W$ = `width`, $H$ = `height`, $B_X$ = `config.items_per_shard`, $C$ = `config.clips_dir`, $Q_c$ = `config.pair_clips_dir`, $Q_\ell$ = `config.pair_labels_dir` (both null or both strings; the index has pairs iff they are strings), $N_U$ = `counts.train.clips`, $N_R$ = `counts.train.pairs`.
- Training part: $N = N_U + N_R$ items in $S = \lceil N / B_X \rceil$ shards $s = 0..S-1$ of `{X}/train/`; $|I_s| = B_X$ for $s < S - 1$, $|I_{S-1}| = N - (S - 1) B_X$; $I_s$ — the items of its `items.json` in order. The `validation` part is not read.
- Item — `{"clip": p}` (unlabeled) or `{"a": p_a, "b": p_b, "labels": y}` (labeled), as in `specs/train-index.md`; a path $p$ is relative to the repository root.
- Clip at $p$ — `Clip(frames, width, height, info, lines)`: `frames` — `numpy.fromfile` of `{p}/frames.yuv` with `dtype=numpy.uint8`, reshaped to $(L, WH \cdot 3 / 2)$ (row $i$: state frame $i$, yuv420p); `width` = $W$, `height` = $H$; `info` — `json.loads` of the bytes of `{p}/clip.json`; `lines` — the bytes of `{p}/lines.jsonl` split at `b"\n"`, the empty piece after the last `\n` dropped, each piece decoded as UTF-8 and parsed by `json.loads`, in order.
- Mini-batch $\mathcal{B}$ — a list of items of one shard; $K(\mathcal{B})$ — its clips as a list in item order (an unlabeled item: its clip; a labeled item: $a$, then $b$; a clip in several items is in the list once per occurrence); $R(\mathcal{B})$ — its labeled items in order, $r$ with the positions $i_a(r), i_b(r)$ of its clips in $K(\mathcal{B})$ and its labels $y_r$; for $R = R(\mathcal{B})$: $Z_{i_a(R)}$, $Z_{i_b(R)}$ — the rows $i_a(r)$, $i_b(r)$ of $Z$ for $r \in R$ in order, $y_R$ — the list of the $y_r$ in order.
- Totals: $K_{tot} = N_U + 2 N_R$ clips, $R_{tot} = N_R$ pairs; $M = \sum_s \lceil |I_s| / b \rceil$ mini-batches per epoch; $T = EM$ steps.
- Networks (`torch.nn.Module`): $\phi$ — clips $c_1..c_n$ → $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times L \times D}$ ($D$ — $\phi$'s latent size, block 2), row $i$ depending only on $c_i$; $\pi(Z)$ — $\pi$'s outputs for every state of each clip (its action logits and shifts, block 6; which past states each uses is $\pi$'s), row $i$ depending only on $Z_i$; $P(Z_a, Z_b)$ — $(p_\succ, p_{cmp})$ of each pair, $Z_a, Z_b \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times L \times D}$, $m \ge 1$ (block 5; which states of each clip it uses is $P$'s), entry $r$ depending only on pair $r$; $P$ absent iff $N_R = 0$. The networks are called by the loop, as at inference ([U]).
- Losses (`torch.nn.Module`, possibly with parameters, e.g. METRA's dual variable in $\ell^\phi$): $\ell^\phi(Z, \text{clips}) \in \mathbb{R}^n$; $\ell^\pi(\pi(Z), Z, \text{clips}) \in \mathbb{R}^n$; $\ell^P(P(Z_a, Z_b), \text{labels}) \in \mathbb{R}^m$; they never call a network; entry $i$ depending only on clip (pair) $i$; the reduction over the states of a clip, the stop-gradients and the action mask are the components' ([U]).
- Modules, in this order: $\phi$, $\ell^\phi$, $\pi$, $\ell^\pi$, $P$, $\ell^P$, named `phi`, `phi_loss`, `pi`, `pi_loss`, `P`, `P_loss`; required: the first four, plus `P` and `P_loss` iff $N_R \ge 1$ ([U]: no untrained $P$ when no pair is in the training part).
- Groups — the named parameter groups declared by the given modules (`groups()`, Interface), ordered by module order, then by each module's declaration order (the order of AdamW's `param_groups` and of the `lr` keys of `losses.json`); $q$ — a group; $\eta_q$, $\lambda_q$ — its learning rate and weight decay (config); $G$ — every parameter of every group.
- $w_\pi$, $w_P$ — loss weights; $b$ — `batch` (items per mini-batch); $b_\mu$ — `micro_batch` (items per micro-batch); $E$ — `epochs`; $N_c$ — `checkpoint_steps`; $k$ — `keep_checkpoints`; $g_{max}$ — `grad_clip`; $\beta_1, \beta_2$ — `betas`; $\varepsilon$ — `adam_eps`; $T_w$ — `warmup_steps`; $\rho$ — `lr_floor`; $\varpi$ = `math.pi` (the number).
- Config `configs/pretrain.yaml` → `@dataclass(frozen=True) class PretrainConfig`, read with `config_mapping` (a missing or unknown key → `ValueError`), each field as written except as said: `index_dir`, `out_dir` (non-empty relative path strings without a `..` component, by `relative` of `mcpref`); `seed` (`integer` in $[0, 2^{32} - 1]$); `epochs`, `batch`, `micro_batch`, `checkpoint_steps`, `keep_checkpoints` (`positive_int`); `warmup_steps` (`integer` in $[0, \infty]$); `w_pi`, `w_P`, `grad_clip`, `adam_eps` (`number`, > 0); `lr_floor` (`number`, in [0, 1]); `betas` (a list of exactly two `number`s, each in [0, 1), stored as a tuple of floats); `groups` (a mapping from string names to mappings with exactly the keys `lr` (`number`, > 0) and `weight_decay` (`number`, ≥ 0), stored with floats); `device` (a string `torch.device` accepts, by `device` of `training/devices.py`); `number`, `positive_int` and `integer` (a YAML integer, not a boolean, in $[\text{low}, \text{high}]$) of `mcpref` (`number`: a YAML integer or float, not a boolean or a string); violated constraints → `ValueError` at load.

## Math
(1) Loss of a mini-batch, normalised by the whole training part ([U], as `specs/gui-stack.md` (3')): with $Z = \phi(K)$, $K = K(\mathcal{B})$, $R = R(\mathcal{B})$,
$\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{B}) = M \left( \frac{1}{K_{tot}} \sum_{i \in K} \left(\ell^\phi(Z, K)_i + w_\pi\, \ell^\pi(\pi(Z), Z, K)_i\right) + \frac{w_P}{R_{tot}} \sum_{r \in R} \ell^P(P(Z_{i_a(R)}, Z_{i_b(R)}), y_R)_r \right)$,
the second sum 0 when $R = \emptyset$ (always when $N_R = 0$). Its mean over the $M$ mini-batches of an epoch is $\frac{1}{K_{tot}} \sum_{\text{clips}} (\ell^\phi + w_\pi \ell^\pi) + \frac{w_P}{R_{tot}} \sum_{\text{pairs}} \ell^P$ over the training part (the second term absent when $R_{tot} = 0$). No stop-gradient is added here: each term's gradient reaches every parameter it depends on, $\phi$'s included ([U]).
(2) Micro-batches ([U]): $\mathcal{B}$ cut into consecutive runs $\mathcal{B}_1 .. \mathcal{B}_J$ of $b_\mu$ items (the last shorter); for $j = 1..J$ in order: the clips of $K(\mathcal{B}_j)$ read, $Z_j = \phi(K(\mathcal{B}_j))$, $\mathcal{L}_j$ = the terms of (1) over $K(\mathcal{B}_j)$ and $R(\mathcal{B}_j)$ (same factors $M / K_{tot}$, $M w_P / R_{tot}$), the calls in the order $\phi$, $\ell^\phi$, $\pi$, $\ell^\pi$, $P$, $\ell^P$ ([U]; it fixes the draws of the components from torch's global generators), $P$ and $\ell^P$ called only when $R(\mathcal{B}_j) \neq \emptyset$, then $\mathcal{L}_j$`.backward()`, accumulating into the gradients; $\sum_j \mathcal{L}_j = \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{B})$. The gradients are set to `None` (`zero_grad(set_to_none=True)`) before $\mathcal{B}_1$; a parameter not reached by any backward of the step keeps `None`.
(3) Schedule (`specs/gui-stack.md` (4), the functions moved unchanged): $g$ = `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64([seed, 3]))`; per epoch $e = 1..E$: $g$`.permutation(S)` orders the shards, then for each shard $s$ in that order $g$`.permutation(|I_s|)` orders its items, cut into consecutive mini-batches of $b$ positions (the last shorter); $\mathcal{B}$ = the items of $I_s$ at those positions, in that order; steps $t = 1..T$ in schedule order.
(4) Clipping, after the micro-batches of step $t$: `clip_grad_norm_` over $G$ with `max_norm` $= g_{max}$: every gradient that is not `None` multiplied by $\min\left(g_{max} / (\lVert \nabla \rVert_2 + 10^{-6}), 1\right)$, $\lVert \nabla \rVert_2$ the 2-norm of all of them as one vector [T].
(5) Learning rate of group $q$ at step $t = 1..T$: $\eta_{q,t} = \eta_q f(t)$, $f(t) = t / T_w$ for $t \le T_w$, else $f(t) = \rho + (1 - \rho)\, \frac{1}{2} \left(1 + \cos\left(\varpi\, \frac{t - T_w}{T - T_w}\right)\right)$; set as the group's `lr` before the update of step $t$.
(6) Update of step $t$: AdamW per group: for each parameter $\theta$ of group $q$ whose gradient $\nabla$ is not `None`: $\theta \leftarrow \theta (1 - \eta_{q,t} \lambda_q)$, then $m \leftarrow \beta_1 m + (1 - \beta_1) \nabla$, $v \leftarrow \beta_2 v + (1 - \beta_2) \nabla^2$, $\theta \leftarrow \theta - \eta_{q,t} \frac{m / (1 - \beta_1^{n})}{\sqrt{v} / \sqrt{1 - \beta_2^{n}} + \varepsilon}$, $n$ the number of updates of $\theta$ so far, this one included; a parameter whose gradient is `None` keeps its value and state [T], [A].
(7) Logged losses of step $t$: $\bar\ell^\phi = \frac{1}{|K|} \sum_{i \in K} \ell^\phi_i$, $\bar\ell^\pi = \frac{1}{|K|} \sum_{i \in K} \ell^\pi_i$ (over $K = K(\mathcal{B})$), $\bar\ell^P = \frac{1}{|R|} \sum_{r \in R} \ell^P_r$ (null when $R = \emptyset$), $\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{B})$; each accumulated over the micro-batches as Python floats from `.item()` of the detached sums.

## Domain
- One run at a time; no run of train-index on $X$, of perception-data on $C$ or $Q_c$, of the producer of $Q_\ell$ during a run; the inputs well formed (written by their producers; `frames.yuv` of $L W H \cdot 3/2$ bytes).
- The modules satisfy the protocol (Interface) on `device`; their outputs finite; the configured devices hold them.

## Behaviour
(R) Checks, in this order, each aborting with a `RuntimeError` (paths as written in the configs):
1. `finished_run(REPO / X, X, "complete")` (`{X} is not complete`).
2. $N \ge 1$ (`{X} has no training item`).
3. The keys of `modules` equal the required module names (Notation) as sets (`{X} needs the modules {required}, given {given}`, `required` the list of the required names in module order, `given` the list of the keys of `modules` in their order).
4. The groups, over all given modules at once ([U]), the checks in this order: the `groups()` of each module in module order, a name already declared by an earlier module or group (`group {name} declared twice`); scanning the groups in group order and each group's list in order, the first parameter (compared by identity) already seen: in the same group (`a parameter is twice in the group {q}`), else in an earlier group $q'$ (`a parameter is in the groups {q'} and {q}`); a parameter of a module (`parameters()`) in no group, the first module in module order (`{name} has parameters outside the groups`, `name` its module name); the declared names equal the config's `groups` keys as sets (`the modules declare the groups {declared}, the config {configured}`, both lists, in group order and in config order).
5. `finished_run(REPO / D, D, "clips")` of $C$, then, with pairs, of $Q_c$ (`{D} is not complete`), both before the bar ([U]); then the fingerprints of $C$, then $Q_c$, then $Q_\ell$ (with pairs) by `fingerprint` (bar `pretrain inputs`), the fields of $C$ and $Q_c$ by `clip_fields` of those `run.json`; then compared in the order `clips`, `pair_clips`, `pair_labels` with `sha256` of `{X}/run.json` (`{name} changed since {X} was built`).
6. `check_fingerprints({out_dir}/run.json, {"config": sha256 of the config file, "index": sha256 of {X}/run.json}, out_dir)`; then `{out_dir}/run.json` = {`config` (the config file's mapping as written), `sha256`: {`config`, `index`}} by `write_json`.
(T) Training. When `{out_dir}/model.pt` exists: the line `pretrain: already done`. Otherwise:
1. Every module `.train()`; $h$ = `complete_shards` of `{out_dir}/checkpoints/`; with $h \ge 1$, `prune(checkpoints, h - 1, k)`: the `checkpoint.pt` of each checkpoint $0 .. h - 1 - k$ that still has one deleted (`os.unlink`, then `sync` of its directory) — this block's own rule for its checkpoint shards ([U]).
2. $h = 0$: `torch.manual_seed(seed)`; AdamW built over the groups (6); step 0, epoch 1, `schedule_rng` null, `batch_in_epoch` 0. $h \ge 1$: `checkpoint.pt` of checkpoint $h - 1$ loaded by `torch.load(path, map_location="cpu")`; each module's `load_state_dict(modules[name])`, the AdamW built as for $h = 0$, then its `load_state_dict(optimizer)`; `torch.set_rng_state(torch_rng)`, and on CUDA `torch.cuda.set_rng_state(cuda_rng, device)`; the position (step, epoch, `schedule_rng`, `batch_in_epoch`) taken.
3. Steps by `remaining` (3) from the position: `items.json` of a shard read once per visit (`json.loads`, key `items`); per step (2), (4), (5), (6), (7); after the update of step $t$, checkpoint $j$ (the number of checkpoints so far) written when $N_c \mid t$ or $t = T$, then `prune(checkpoints, j, k)`; the bar advanced. A restored step $T$: the line `pretrain: already done` instead of the bar.
4. `{out_dir}/model.pt` written: `torch.save` of {module name: CPU copy of its `state_dict()`} for the given modules to `model.pt.part` (`part_path`), then `publish`.
Finally, on both paths, `{out_dir}/run.json` rewritten by `write_json` with `"complete": true` added.

## Interface
- Protocol, implemented by blocks 2, 6, 5 (each module a `torch.nn.Module` already on `device`, built and initialised by the caller):
  - `phi(clips: list[Clip]) -> Tensor` — $Z$, shape $(n, L, D)$;
  - `pi(Z: Tensor) -> object` — $\pi(Z)$, of the type block 6 sets; `P(Z_a: Tensor, Z_b: Tensor) -> object` — $P(Z_a, Z_b)$, of the type block 5 sets;
  - `phi_loss(Z: Tensor, clips: list[Clip]) -> Tensor`, `pi_loss(out: object, Z: Tensor, clips: list[Clip]) -> Tensor` (`out` = $\pi(Z)$) — shape $(n,)$;
  - `P_loss(out: object, labels: list[dict]) -> Tensor` (`out` = $P(Z_a, Z_b)$) — shape $(m,)$, $m \ge 1$;
  - every module: `groups() -> dict[str, list[torch.nn.Parameter]]` — its named groups, together holding each of its `parameters()` once (`{}` without parameters); all its state in `state_dict()`; randomness only from torch's global CPU and CUDA generators.
