## Precision
Fresh runs use full BF16 parameter storage: all model and dual parameters, accumulated gradients, and AdamW `exp_avg`/`exp_avg_sq` tensors are bfloat16, with no FP32 master weights. Adam step counters retain the library FP32 dtype. Seeded initialization/scaling precedes conversion; internal Qwen stability operations and detached diagnostic reductions may use higher precision. This applies to joint training and frozen-perception retraining. New checkpoints preserve BF16 and resume in the same precision; conversion of legacy runs is outside this change.

With perception `fp8_mlp: true`, only Qwen text-MLP forward and input-gradient GEMMs use FP8; weight-gradient GEMMs and all stored training state remain BF16. Scope, recipe, checkpointing and validation limits are in `specs/perception.md`, Precision. A fresh run is required when the perception config fingerprint changes.

## Math
(1) Loss of a mini-batch, normalised by the whole training part ([U], as `specs/gui-stack.md` (3')): with $Z = \phi(K)$, $K = K(\mathcal{B})$, $R = R(\mathcal{B})$,
$\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{B}) = M \left( \frac{1}{K_{tot}} \sum_{i \in K} \left(w_\phi\,\ell^\phi(Z, K)_i + w_\pi\, \ell^\pi(\pi(Z, K), Z, K)_i\right) + \frac{w_P}{R_{tot}} \sum_{r \in R} \ell^P(P(Z_{i_a(R)}, Z_{i_b(R)}), y_R)_r \right)$,
the second sum 0 when $R = \emptyset$ (always when $N_R = 0$). Its mean over the $M$ mini-batches of an epoch is $\frac{1}{K_{tot}} \sum_{\text{clips}} (w_\phi \ell^\phi + w_\pi \ell^\pi) + \frac{w_P}{R_{tot}} \sum_{\text{pairs}} \ell^P$ over the training part (the second term absent when $R_{tot} = 0$). No stop-gradient is added here: each term's gradient reaches every parameter it depends on, $\phi$'s included ([U]); `w_phi = 0` removes only the own $\ell^\phi$ contribution, not the paths from $\ell^\pi$ or $\ell^P$ to $\phi$.
(2) Micro-batches ([U]): $\mathcal{B}$ cut into consecutive runs $\mathcal{B}_1 .. \mathcal{B}_J$ of $b_\mu$ items (the last shorter); for $j = 1..J$ in order: the clips of $K(\mathcal{B}_j)$ read, $Z_j = \phi(K(\mathcal{B}_j))$, $\mathcal{L}_j$ = the terms of (1) over $K(\mathcal{B}_j)$ and $R(\mathcal{B}_j)$ (same factors $M / K_{tot}$, $M w_P / R_{tot}$), the calls in the order $\phi$, $\ell^\phi$, $\pi$, $\ell^\pi$, $P$, $\ell^P$ ([U]; it fixes the draws of the components from torch's global generators), $P$ and $\ell^P$ called only when $R(\mathcal{B}_j) \neq \emptyset$, then $\mathcal{L}_j$`.backward()`, accumulating into the gradients; $\sum_j \mathcal{L}_j = \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{B})$. The first micro-batch's clips are read synchronously in path order. Before each current micro-batch's forward pass, one worker may read only the immediate next micro-batch's clips in path order, with at most one outstanding future. Its result is consumed at the next micro-batch's normal clip-read point, after the current backward, and any read error propagates there; only current and next clips are retained. This overlap does not change micro-batch, backward, RNG or shard order. On ordinary CUDA steps, the `PhiLoss` and `PreferenceLoss` tensor cores may use their compiled interfaces; selected gradient-flow steps use their eager interfaces. Policy/preference networks and policy loss remain eager. With `fp8_mlp`, Qwen text MLPs also use compiled interfaces on ordinary CUDA steps; the remainder of the backbone stays eager. Selected diagnostics force the complete forward/backward into eager mode to preserve buffers for repeated gradients. Call order is unchanged. The gradients are set to `None` (`zero_grad(set_to_none=True)`) before $\mathcal{B}_1$; a parameter not reached by any backward of the step keeps `None`.
Perception batching: $b_\mu$ counts training items. An unlabeled item contributes one clip and a preference item two, so one micro-batch sends $|K(\mathcal B_j)|\le 2b_\mu$ independent rows to Qwen in one forward (`specs/perception.md` (6)). Increasing `micro_batch` therefore increases the actual perception batch; loss factors, gradient accumulation and the optimizer-step batch remain unchanged.

(3) Schedule (`specs/gui-stack.md` (4), the functions moved unchanged): $g$ = `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64([seed, 3]))`; per epoch $e = 1..E$: $g$`.permutation(S)` orders the shards, then for each shard $s$ in that order $g$`.permutation(|I_s|)` orders its items, cut into consecutive mini-batches of $b$ positions (the last shorter); $\mathcal{B}$ = the items of $I_s$ at those positions, in that order; steps $t = 1..T$ in schedule order.
(4) Clipping, after the micro-batches of step $t$: `clip_grad_norm_` over $G$ with `max_norm` $= g_{max}$: every gradient that is not `None` multiplied by $\min\left(g_{max} / (\lVert \nabla \rVert_2 + 10^{-6}), 1\right)$, $\lVert \nabla \rVert_2$ the 2-norm of all of them as one vector [T].
(5) Learning rate of group $q$ at step $t = 1..T$: $\eta_{q,t} = \eta_q f(t)$, $f(t) = t / T_w$ for $t \le T_w$, else $f(t) = \rho + (1 - \rho)\, \frac{1}{2} \left(1 + \cos\left(\varpi\, \frac{t - T_w}{T - T_w}\right)\right)$; set as the group's `lr` before the update of step $t$.
(6) Update of step $t$: AdamW per group: for each parameter $\theta$ of group $q$ whose gradient $\nabla$ is not `None`: $\theta \leftarrow \theta (1 - \eta_{q,t} \lambda_q)$, then $m \leftarrow \beta_1 m + (1 - \beta_1) \nabla$, $v \leftarrow \beta_2 v + (1 - \beta_2) \nabla^2$, $\theta \leftarrow \theta - \eta_{q,t} \frac{m / (1 - \beta_1^{n})}{\sqrt{v} / \sqrt{1 - \beta_2^{n}} + \varepsilon}$, $n$ the number of updates of $\theta$ so far, this one included; a parameter whose gradient is `None` keeps its value and state [T], [A].
(7) Logged losses of step $t$: the existing $\bar\ell^\phi$, $\bar\ell^\pi$, $\bar\ell^P$ (null when $R=\emptyset$) and $\mathcal L(\mathcal B)$ retain their definitions and accumulation as Python floats from detached sums. Detached loss/component scalars remain on the configured device until after the last micro-batch backward, then one `torch.stack(...).cpu().tolist()` transfer supplies values in the original micro-batch order for the same Python-float accumulation; there is no host transfer between forward and backward. The step record additionally contains `counts`, `components`, `outputs`, `phi_stats`, `gradients`, `updates`, `baselines` exactly as in `specs/pretrain-diagnostics.md` Step JSON; the comparable-only `succ` output suite and nullable infinite `copy_current` follow that spec. One hundred-step aggregates are written to `diagnostics.json` with its exact schema and `WindowAccumulator.drain(final=(step==T))` behaviour.

## Behaviour
(R) Checks, in this order, each aborting with a `RuntimeError` (paths as written in the configs):
1. `finished_run(REPO / X, X, "complete")` (`{X} is not complete`).
2. $N \ge 1$ (`{X} has no training item`).
3. The keys of `modules` equal the required module names (Notation) as sets (`{X} needs the modules {required}, given {given}`, `required` the list of the required names in module order, `given` the list of the keys of `modules` in their order).
4. The groups, over all given modules at once ([U]), the checks in this order: the `groups()` of each module in module order, a name already declared by an earlier module or group (`group {name} declared twice`); scanning the groups in group order and each group's list in order, the first parameter (compared by identity) already seen: in the same group (`a parameter is twice in the group {q}`), else in an earlier group $q'$ (`a parameter is in the groups {q'} and {q}`); a parameter of a module (`parameters()`) in no group, the first module in module order (`{name} has parameters outside the groups`, `name` its module name); the declared names equal the config's `groups` keys as sets (`the modules declare the groups {declared}, the config {configured}`, both lists, in group order and in config order).
5. `finished_run(REPO / D, D, "clips")` of $C$, then, with pairs, of $Q_c$ (`{D} is not complete`), both before the bar ([U]); then the fingerprints of $C$, then $Q_c$, then $Q_\ell$ (with pairs) by `fingerprint` (bar `pretrain inputs`), the fields of $C$ and $Q_c$ by `clip_fields` of those `run.json`; then compared in the order `clips`, `pair_clips`, `pair_labels` with `sha256` of `{X}/run.json` (`{name} changed since {X} was built`).

(T) Training. When `{out_dir}/model.pt` exists: the line `pretrain: already done`. Otherwise:
1. Every module `.train()`; $h$ = `complete_shards` of `{out_dir}/checkpoints/`; with $h \ge 1$, `prune(checkpoints, h - 1, k)`: the `checkpoint.pt` of each checkpoint $0 .. h - 1 - k$ that still has one deleted (`os.unlink`, then `sync` of its directory) — this block's own rule for its checkpoint shards ([U]).
2. $h = 0$: `torch.manual_seed(seed)`; AdamW built over the groups (6); step 0, epoch 1, `schedule_rng` null, `batch_in_epoch` 0. $h \ge 1$: `checkpoint.pt` of checkpoint $h - 1$ loaded by `torch.load(path, map_location="cpu")`; each module's `load_state_dict(modules[name])`, the AdamW built as for $h = 0$, then its `load_state_dict(optimizer)`; `torch.set_rng_state(torch_rng)`, and on CUDA `torch.cuda.set_rng_state(cuda_rng, device)`; the position (step, epoch, `schedule_rng`, `batch_in_epoch`) taken.
3. Steps by `remaining` (3) from the position: `items.json` of a shard read once per visit (`json.loads`, key `items`); per step (2), (4), (5), (6), (7) and the diagnostic observations of `specs/pretrain-diagnostics.md`; after the update of step $t$, checkpoint $j$ written when $N_c \mid t$ or $t = T$, then `prune(checkpoints, j, k)`; the bar advanced. Selected gradient-flow steps use `backward(retain_graph=True)` only for returned component gradients, restore post-original-backward RNG and preserve the original update and stored training results. Pending gradient-flow records are accepted only inside the next published checkpoint, and `gradient-flow/summary.json` is regenerated after publication. Reference evaluation follows checkpoint-first ordering of `specs/pretrain-diagnostics.md` (21). A restored step $T$: the line `pretrain: already done` instead of the bar.
4. `{out_dir}/model.pt` written: `torch.save` of {module name: CPU copy of its `state_dict()`} for the given modules to `model.pt.part` (`part_path`), then `publish`.
(R2) Optional retraining. If `retrain` is true and `{out_dir}/retrain/run.json` is not complete, run a second phase after (T), before the outer `run.json` is marked complete. With no retrain checkpoint, first load the joint `model.pt` into the modules, then freeze every parameter of `phi` and `phi_loss`; preserve the current `pi,P` weights. Build a fresh AdamW over exactly the parameter groups belonging to `pi,P`, reset torch's seed and the LR/epoch schedule, and run the same $T$ steps over the same items and fixed preference labels. The forward losses retain (1), but no gradient or update reaches `phi` or `phi_loss`. Write per-step/window records and resumable checkpoints with the joint structure under `retrain/checkpoints/`; do not emit new global diagnostic-runtime events. A retrain checkpoint restores its modules, heads-only optimizer, RNG and exact schedule position. At $T$, atomically replace the outer `model.pt` with the frozen `phi` and retrained heads, then publish `retrain/run.json` = `{complete:true,steps:T}`. A completed marker skips the phase; an interrupted final publication is reconciled from the last retrain checkpoint.
Finally, on both paths, `{out_dir}/run.json` rewritten by `write_json` with `"complete": true` added.
