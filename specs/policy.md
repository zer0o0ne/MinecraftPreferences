## Notation
- Clip, $L$, states $s = 0..L-1$, intervals $s = 0..L-2$ (from state $s$ to $s + 1$), $z_s$ = row $s$ of the clip's $Z$: as in `specs/perception.md`; $\mathcal{A}_s$ = `info["actions"][s]` — the action names of interval $s$ in the order of `specs/vpt-actions.md` (8), with repeats (e.g. several turns); $n_s$ = `info["n"][s]` = $|\mathcal{A}_s|$.
- $A$ — `Masks.names` of `specs/action-mask.md` ($|A| = 395$), $\iota(a)$ — the index of action $a$ in it; $\varnothing$ — "no action", index $|A|$ of the input weights; $M_s \in \{0, 1\}^{|A|}$ — row $s$ of `clip_masks(masks, info, lines)` (1 = allowed).
- $D$ — the latent size, `len(alpha)` of `configs/perception.yaml`, given by the caller; $d_\alpha$ — perception's metric, given to $\ell^\pi$ as a function $\delta \mapsto \lVert \alpha \odot \delta \rVert_2$ (`latent_metric`, revision of `specs/perception.md`); $\pi$ and $\ell^\pi$ never hold $\alpha$ [U].
- History — latents $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times \ell \times D}$ and input weights $C \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times \ell \times (|A| + 1)}$, rows oldest first, $m \ge 1$, $\ell \ge 1$; $c_j$ — the weights of the action executed in the interval starting at row $j$ (non-negative, summing to 1); $L_\pi$ — `history`; $n = \min(L_\pi, \ell)$ — the used rows $Y_{:, \ell - n : \ell}$, $C_{:, \ell - n : \ell}$; the position of used row $j = 0..n-1$: $\varpi_j = j - (n - 1)$. In pretraining $Y = Z$ of `specs/pretrain.md`, $C$ by (1), $\ell = L = L_\pi$ (Domain).
- $d$ — `width`; $h$ — `heads`, $d_h = d / h$ (even); $f$ — `hidden`; $N_\pi$ — `layers` (their number); $f_\Delta$ — `shift_hidden`; $\vartheta$ — `rope_base`; $\epsilon$ — `norm_eps`; $p_\varnothing$ — `action_dropout`; $w_\Delta$ — `shift_weight`; $\varsigma$ — `seed`.
- Parameters, all float32 (float64 in the tests): $W_{in} \in \mathbb{R}^{D \times d}$; $W_c \in \mathbb{R}^{(|A| + 1) \times d}$ (executed actions); per layer $\gamma_1, W_q, W_k, W_v, W_o, \gamma_2, W_1, W_3, W_2$ of `specs/preference.md` Notation; the final gain $\gamma_f$; the logit matrix $W_\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times |A|}$; the action embeddings $E \in \mathbb{R}^{|A| \times d}$, row $e_a$; the shift MLP's gain $\gamma_\Delta \in \mathbb{R}^{2d}$ and $U_1, U_3 \in \mathbb{R}^{2d \times f_\Delta}$, $U_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{f_\Delta \times D}$. A matrix acts on row vectors.
- $\mathrm{N}_\gamma$, $\mathrm{R}$, $\mathrm{F}$ — RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU of `specs/preference.md`; $\hat H = \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_1}(H)$ — a layer's normed input; $\mathrm{sg}$ — stop-gradient (`detach`).
- $r_j$ — the readout of used row $j$ (4); $\lambda^\pi_j \in \mathbb{R}^{|A|}$ — its logits (5); $\widehat{\Delta z}_j(a) \in \mathbb{R}^D$ — the predicted shift of action $a$ at row $j$, in $z$ (6); $a^\to_s$ — the action whose shift $\pi$ computes at clip state $s$ (7); $q^\pi_s$, $\chi^\pi_s$ — the targets of state $s$ (8) (the superscript keeps them apart from $\lambda$, $\chi_s$, $q_s$ of `specs/perception.md`).
- Config `configs/policy.yaml` → `@dataclass(frozen=True) class PolicyConfig`, fields in this order, read with `config_mapping` (a missing or unknown key → `ValueError`), violated constraints → `ValueError` at load: `history`, `width`, `heads`, `hidden`, `layers`, `shift_hidden` (`positive_int`; `width` divisible by `heads` with an even quotient); `rope_base`, `norm_eps` (`positive`); `action_dropout` (`number`, in $[0, 1)$); `shift_weight` (`positive`); `seed` (`integer` in $[0, 2^{32} - 1]$); `latency_ms`, `latency_fail_ms` (`positive`); `latency_calls` (`positive_int`); `positive_int`, `positive` (a `number` > 0), `number`, `integer` of `mcpref`.

## Math
(1) Input weights of a clip [U]: for state $j = 0..L-2$, $c_j(a)$ = (the count of $a$ in $\mathcal{A}_j$) $/ n_j$ for $a \in A$ and $c_j(\varnothing) = 0$ — every action of the interval, masked or not (it was executed); $c_{L-1}$ = the one-hot of $\varnothing$ (its interval lies outside the clip). At RL $C$ is given by the caller.
(2) Tokens: with the used rows, in training mode (`self.training`) after the dropout $b = $ `torch.rand((m, n), device=<Y's device>, dtype=torch.float32)` $< p_\varnothing$ (one draw per call from the global generator of that device), $c_j \leftarrow$ the one-hot of $\varnothing$ where $b_j$ [U]; $H^0_j = y_j W_{in} + c_j W_c$ ($c_j W_c$ = the frequency-weighted mean of the rows of $W_c$). No dropout in eval mode.
(3) Layer C, causal [U]: $H \leftarrow H + \mathrm{Attn}_c(\hat H, \hat H)$, $\hat H = \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_1}(H)$, then $H \leftarrow H + \mathrm{F}(\mathrm{N}_{\gamma_2}(H))$; $\mathrm{Attn}_c$ — (3) of `specs/preference.md` with positions $\varpi$ on both sides and the sum over $j' = 0..j$ only: $o_{j, \eta} = \sum_{j' = 0}^{j} \mathrm{softmax}_{j'}\left(q_j \cdot \kappa_{j'} / \sqrt{d_h}\right) v_{j'}$.
(4) Stack: $H^0$ through $N_\pi$ layers C, each with its own parameters; $r_j = \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_f}(H_{:, j})$ for every used row $j$. Row $j$ depends only on rows $0..j$; without dropout it equals the readout of the last row of the history $(Y, C)_{:, 0 : j + 1}$ alone (RoPE depends only on $\varpi_j - \varpi_{j'}$), so one pass over a clip gives every state's output.
(5) Logits [U]: $\lambda^\pi_j = r_j W_\lambda$ (no bias); $\pi(\cdot \mid \text{row } j) = \mathrm{softmax}(\lambda^\pi_j)$.
(6) Shift of action $a$ at row $j$ [U], [S1]: $x = \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_\Delta}([r_j ; e_a]) \in \mathbb{R}^{2d}$, $\widehat{\Delta z}_j(a) = \left(\mathrm{SiLU}(x U_1) \odot x U_3\right) U_2$ — in $z$ [U].
(7) Output in pretraining, $\pi(Z, \text{clips})$: $\lambda^\pi$ of shape $(m, L, |A|)$ and $\widehat{\Delta z}_s(a^\to_s)$ of shape $(m, L, D)$, with $a^\to_s$ = the action of $\mathcal{A}_{s+1}$ when $s \le L - 3$ and $n_{s+1} = 1$, else index 0 (a placeholder whose output (9) never uses) [U].
(8) Targets of clip state $s = 0..L-3$ (Q MCTS-10; interval $s + 1$ is executed from state $s + 1$): $\mathcal{T}_s$ — the actions of $\mathcal{A}_{s+1}$ with $M_{s+1}[\iota(a)] = 1$, repeats kept (`specs/action-mask.md`); $n'_s = |\mathcal{T}_s|$; $q^\pi_s(a)$ = (the count of $a$ in $\mathcal{T}_s$) $/ n'_s$, $q^\pi_s = 0$ when $n'_s = 0$ [U]; $\chi^\pi_s = [n_s = 1]\,[n_{s+1} = 1]\,[n'_s = 1]$ [U] (then $\mathcal{T}_s = \mathcal{A}_{s+1} = (a^\to_s)$).
(9) Loss of a clip [U], with $z_s$ = row $s$ of its $Z$ and its outputs $(\lambda^\pi, \widehat{\Delta z})$ of (7):
$\ell^\pi = \frac{1}{L - 2} \sum_{s=0}^{L-3} \left[ \mathrm{CE}(\lambda^\pi_s, q^\pi_s) + w_\Delta\, \chi^\pi_s\, d_\alpha\!\left(\widehat{\Delta z}_s(a^\to_s) - \mathrm{sg}(z_{s+1} - z_s)\right)^2 \right]$,
$\mathrm{CE}(\lambda, q) = -\sum_{a \in A} q(a) \log \mathrm{softmax}(\lambda)_a$ (0 when $q = 0$), computed on the rows $(m (L - 2), |A|)$; the shift term with $\chi^\pi_s = 0$ removed by `torch.where`; in the dtype of $\lambda^\pi$, the targets built on its device and in its dtype; shape $(m,)$, $m$ the clips.
(10) Initialisation: as `specs/preference.md` (11): built on the CPU inside `torch.random.fork_rng(devices=[])` after `torch.random.default_generator.manual_seed(`$\varsigma$`)`, the submodules created in the attribute order of the Interface; torch's defaults: every `Linear` $\mathcal{U}(\pm 1/\sqrt{\text{fan\_in}})$ ($W_c$ too, [U]), $E$ $\mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ [T], every gain 1; no dropout but (2). The caller moves $\pi$ to its device.
(11) Groups: the HF Trainer's rule of `specs/perception.md` (12) for $\pi$'s parameters: $\pi$ declares, in this order, `pi` — the weights of its `Linear`s and $E$, and `pi_no_decay` — the gains, each list in `named_parameters()` order; $\ell^\pi$ declares none.
(12) Latency (soft, as `specs/preference.md` (13)) [U]: one call = `readout` of $(Y, C)$ with $m = 1$, $\ell = L_\pi$, then `logits` of its last row and one batched `shift` of that row for every action of $A$ — the readout repeated to $(|A|, d)$, $a$ = `torch.arange(|A|)` (an upper bound of an MCTS call on $l \le |A|$ actions) [U]; the median over `latency_calls` calls, after `latency_calls` warm-up calls, at most `latency_ms` on the Spark's GPU; eval mode, float32, `torch.no_grad`; after `torch.manual_seed(0)` $Y$ = `torch.randn(1, L_π, D, device=<the GPU>)`, $C$ the one-hot of $\varnothing$ in every row, $D$ = `len(alpha)` of `configs/perception.yaml`, $|A|$ from `configs/action-mask.yaml`; each call timed by `time.perf_counter` with `torch.cuda.synchronize()` before starting and before stopping; the `gpu` test prints the median and fails only above `latency_fail_ms`.
(13) `main` of the pretraining loop [U]: `python -m mcpref.pretrain.run <config>` (argparse, one positional path; bad arguments → argparse's exit 2): the `PretrainConfig` at the path, then the configs it names, in the order `perception`, `action_mask` (with `load_masks`), `policy`, `preference` (each by its block's `load_config` on `REPO / <path>`); then `index_run(cfg)` (`specs/pretrain.md` (R) 1, 2); with $L$ = `states` of that `run.json`, $X$ = `index_dir` as written and pairs iff `counts.train.pairs` $\ge 1$, in this order: $L \ge 3$, else `RuntimeError(f"{X} has clips of {L} states, the policy needs at least 3")`; $L_\pi = L$, else `RuntimeError(f"the policy history {L_π} differs from the clip length {L} of {X}")`; with pairs $L_P \le L$, else `RuntimeError(f"the preference history {L_P} exceeds the clip length {L} of {X}")`; then the modules, in module order, each moved to `torch.device(cfg.device)`: `phi` = `build_phi(perception)`, `phi_loss` = `PhiLoss(perception)`, `pi` = `Policy(policy, len(perception.alpha), masks.names)`, `pi_loss` = `PolicyLoss(policy, masks, latent_metric(perception))`, with pairs `P` = `Preference(preference, len(perception.alpha))`, `P_loss` = `PreferenceLoss()`; then `pretrain(cfg, path, modules)`; exit 0; an exception of `FAILURES` → `failed("mcpref.pretrain.run", error)` (exit 1).

## Domain
- In pretraining $L \ge 3$ and $L_\pi = L$ (checked by (13)); every name of $\mathcal{A}_s$ in $A$; inputs finite, on $\pi$'s device, in its dtype; $C$ rows non-negative, summing to 1; clips as `specs/perception-data.md`, each state's mask inputs in the domain of `specs/action-mask.md`. At RL a history of $L_\pi$ latents is two longer than the longest trained one ($L - 2$, the last state with a target), accepted [U].
