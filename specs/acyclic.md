## Math
(1) Verdict of a comparison $(a, b)$ ([U]): valid — its labels that are tokens of `weights`; $n_v$ — their count; $n_r$ — the valid labels whose weight is null. $n_v = 0$ → `dropped`. $n_r / n_v > \tau$ → `incomparable`. Else $p = \frac{1}{n_v - n_r} \sum \tilde\pi$ over the valid labels with a weight ($n_v - n_r \ge 1$ since $\tau < 1$), $\tilde\pi = \pi(t)$ for order 0 and $1 - \pi(t)$ for order 1; $p \ge 1/2$ → `comparable` with $(u, v, w) = (a, b, p)$; $p < 1/2$ → `comparable` with $(u, v, w) = (b, a, 1 - p)$.
(2) $F_0$ = the arc ids returned by `igraph.Graph(n=<vertices>, edges=<arcs in order>, directed=True).feedback_arc_set(weights=[float(c_e) in arc order], method="eades")` on the whole $G$ ([U]), the steps (i)–(iv) of [I] with igraph's float64 semantics (ties included) as the definition.
(3) Put back ([U]): the arcs of $F_0$ in order of descending $c_e$, ties by ascending arc id; each, $u \to v$, leaves the removed set iff $G$ without the arcs still removed has no directed path from $v$ to $u$ (checked after the earlier arcs of this order were handled). $F$ — the arcs still removed at the end; $G \setminus F$ is acyclic and no arc of $F$ can be added to it without a cycle.
(3') Computation: $G$ has no arc between the clips of two shards, so (2) and (3) on $G$ equal their union over $k$ on the subgraph of shard $k$ alone, its vertices and arcs numbered in the same relative order ((2): [I]; (3): a path never leaves a shard's clips, and the put-back order restricted to a shard is the same); each shard's subgraph is built when the shard is read.
(4) Labels of a comparison: `comparable`, arc $\notin F$ → `{"p": float(w), "inc": 0}`; `comparable`, arc $\in F$ → `{"p": null, "inc": 1}` ([U]: removed arcs become incomparable); `incomparable` → `{"p": null, "inc": 1}`; `dropped` → no pair. The pair is $(u, v)$ for a comparable verdict, $(a, b)$ otherwise.
(5) Contract for P's loss (block preference, [U]): for every pair $(a, b, \text{labels})$, $\mathrm{BCE}(p_{inc}(a, b), \text{inc}) + \mathrm{BCE}(p_{inc}(b, a), \text{inc})$; for a pair with `inc` = 0 also $\mathrm{BCE}(p_\succ(a, b), p) + \mathrm{BCE}(p_\succ(b, a), 1 - p)$; no $p_\succ$ term for `inc` = 1 ($p_\succ$ is the preference given comparability).

## Behaviour
(R) Checks, in this order, each failing one aborting with a `RuntimeError` (paths as written in the config): `{V_ℓ}/run.json` holds `complete` (`finished_run`, marker `complete`; `{V_ℓ} is not complete`); the keys of `weights`, in order, equal the `token`s of `config.answers` of `{V_ℓ}/run.json`, in order (`weights of {config path} differ from the answers of {V_ℓ}`).
(I) Inputs: `votes` = `fingerprint(votes_dir, "answers.json", None, update)` (`runs/files.py`): the lowercase hex SHA-256 of the `answers.json` bytes of the complete shards of `{V_ℓ}/shards/` in shard order, read one at a time (bar `correction inputs`). Then `check_fingerprints` of an existing `{out_dir}/run.json` in the order `config`, `votes`, `pair_clips`; then `{out_dir}/run.json` = {`config` (the config file's mapping as written), `sha256`: {`config` (`sha256` of the config file), `votes`}, `pair_clips`: `sha256.pool` of `{V_ℓ}/run.json`} is written by `write_json`.
(W) For $k = h..K-1$: shard $k$ of $V_\ell$ read; (1), then (2) and (3) by (3'), then (4) for its comparisons; shard $k$ of `{out_dir}/shards/` written by `write_shard` with `pairs.json` = `json.dumps([...], indent=2) + "\n"`, one entry per comparison of shard $k$ in order except `dropped`: {`a`: {`shard`: $k$, `id`, `episode`}, `b`: likewise, `labels`: (4)}, and `counts.json` = `json.dumps({"comparable": <arcs not in F>, "removed": <arcs in F>, "incomparable": <incomparable verdicts>, "dropped": <dropped verdicts>}, indent=2) + "\n"` (bar `correction shards`). Then `{out_dir}/run.json` is rewritten by `write_json` with `"complete": true` and `counts` = {`pairs` (comparable + removed + incomparable), then the four sums} over the `counts.json` of all shards, read one at a time (no bar: small files), added after it.
