## Notation
- $Q_c$ — `pool_dir`, a complete output of block preference-pool; $K$ — its complete shards; shard $k$: its clips and its comparisons $(a, b)$, in the order of its `comparisons.json`; $a$, $b$ — clip references `{"id", "episode"}` of shard $k$.
- $W$, $H$, $L$ — `config.width`, `config.height`, `config.states` of `{Q_c}/run.json`.
- Clip $x$ of shard $k$: the directory `{Q_c}/shards/<name(k)>/clips/<x.id>/`; frame $j$ ($0 \le j < L$) — bytes $[j\beta, (j + 1)\beta)$ of its `frames.yuv`, $\beta = W H \cdot 3/2$, as RGB by `yuv420p_to_rgb(·, W, H)`; $\lambda_x$ — the last line of its `lines.jsonl` (the line of its last state, the compared state), parsed by `json.loads`.
- $k_V$ — `frames`, $s_V$ — `stride`; shown frames of $x$: $j_q = L - 1 - (k_V - 1 - q)\, s_V$, $q = 0..k_V - 1$ (oldest first).
- $\mathcal{A}$ — `answers`: a list of entries {`token`, `description`} (the categories and the refusal; their weights: `specs/acyclic.md`).
- $\iota(x)$ — the inventory text of clip $x$, $\theta$ — the question text, both defined in (1).
- $s$ — `samples`. Order $o \in \{0, 1\}$: $o = 0$ shows $(a, b)$, $o = 1$ shows $(b, a)$, as (first, second). Request $(o, i)$ of a comparison, $i = 0..s-1$, in the order $o$, then $i$.
- $m$ — the index of a comparison over the comparisons of shards $0..K-1$ concatenated in shard order; position of request $(o, i)$ of comparison $m$: $2sm + os + i$; its seed: `seed` + its position.
- $h$ — the complete label shards.
- Config `configs/vlm-labels.yaml` → `@dataclass(frozen=True) class VlmLabelsConfig`, fields in this order, read with `config_mapping` (a missing or unknown key → `ValueError`); violated constraints → `ValueError` at load: `pool_dir`, `vlm` (path of `configs/vlm.yaml`), `out_dir` (strings, non-empty, relative to the repository root, no `..` component, checked by `relative` of `mcpref`; kept as written, resolved against the repository root where read or written); `instruction`, `first`, `second`, `question` (contains `{answers}`), `answer_line` (contains `{token}` and `{description}`), `prefix` (none of the characters `` ` ``, `*`, `"`, `'` and no `\n`: (2) removes or splits them), `inventory_line` (contains `{items}`), `item_line` (contains `{type}` and `{quantity}`), `no_items` (strings); `answers` (a non-empty list of mappings with exactly the keys `token`, `description`, both strings, kept as a list of dicts; tokens distinct, non-empty, without whitespace or the characters `` ` ``, `*`, `"`, `'`, `.`, and neither `invalid` nor `no_answer`); `frames` ($k_V \ge 1$), `stride` ($s_V \ge 1$), `samples` ($s \ge 1$) (YAML integers, not booleans); `seed` (a YAML integer, not a boolean, $0 \le$ `seed` $< 2^{32}$).

## Math
(1) Prompt of $(x, y)$ ([U]): the parts [`instruction`, `first`, the shown frames of $x$ in order, $\iota(x)$, `second`, the shown frames of $y$ in order, $\iota(y)$, $\theta$]; $\theta$ = `question` with `{answers}` replaced (`str.replace`) by one `answer_line` per entry of $\mathcal{A}$ in order, `{token}` then `{description}` replaced, joined by `"\n"`. $\iota(x)$ = `inventory_line` with `{items}` replaced by: `no_items` when `inventory` of $\lambda_x$ is empty; else, the entries of `inventory` grouped by `type`, their `quantity` summed, one `item_line` per type in ascending Python string order of `type`, `{type}` then `{quantity}` replaced (the sum as `str`), joined by `", "`.
(2) Label of an answer ([U]): `no_answer` if it is `None`; else every character among `` ` ``, `*`, `"`, `'` (ASCII) removed from the answer; then its last non-empty line (split on `"\n"`, each piece stripped of whitespace, the last non-empty piece; none → `invalid`), one final `.` removed if present, stripped again; it equals (`prefix` + `token`) stripped of whitespace for an entry of $\mathcal{A}$ → that `token`; else `invalid`.

## Domain
- One run at a time; no run of preference-pool on $Q_c$ during a run; the reader (block acyclic) runs while no run does.
- `{out_dir}` holds only what this block writes; every prompt within the server's limits (`max_images` $\ge 2 k_V$, prompt tokens $\le$ `max_prompt_tokens`, `specs/vlm-client.md`); the texts of the config contain no special-token strings (`<|…|>`); not checked.

## Behaviour
(R) Checks, in this order, each failing one aborting with a `RuntimeError` (paths as written in the config): `{Q_c}/run.json` exists and holds `clips` (`finished_run`, marker `clips`; `{Q_c} is not complete`); $(k_V - 1)\, s_V \le L - 1$ (`frames and stride exceed the {L} states of {Q_c}`).
(I) Inputs (bar `label inputs`): `pool` = `fingerprint(pool_dir, "records.json", clip_fields(<{Q_c}/run.json>), update)`, then `comparisons` = `fingerprint(pool_dir, "comparisons.json", None, update)` (the lowercase hex SHA-256 of the `comparisons.json` bytes in shard order), each reading the complete shards one at a time. Then `check_fingerprints` of an existing `{out_dir}/run.json` in the order `config`, `vlm`, `pool`, `comparisons` (`config`, `vlm` — `sha256` of the config file and of the file `vlm`); then `{out_dir}/run.json` = {`config` (the config file's mapping as written), `sha256`: {`config`, `vlm`, `pool`, `comparisons`}} is written by `write_json`.
(L) For $k = h..K-1$: the comparisons of shard $k$, their requests in order (comparison, then $(o, i)$) with the prompts of (1) and the seeds of Notation, asked in one `ask_many` call with the client config of `vlm` (no call when the shard has no comparison); label shard $k$ written by `write_shard` (Datasets); positions continue from `end` of label shard $h - 1$, from 0 when $h = 0$. `no_answer` is final: a rerun never asks again; its calls equal those of an uninterrupted run, the answers may differ (AGENTS.md Data). Then `{out_dir}/run.json` is rewritten by `write_json` with `"complete": true` and `counts` = {`comparisons`, `labels`} added after it: the sums of the `counts.json` of all label shards, read one at a time (no bar: small files, well under a couple of seconds); `labels` keyed in the order of the tokens of $\mathcal{A}$, then `invalid`, `no_answer`, only the labels that occur.
