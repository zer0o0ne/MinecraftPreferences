## Math
(1) Cursor [V2], [U]: $S$ = the top-left $k_c \times k_c$ of the sprite (RGBA `uint8`), $S_{rgb}$ its colour, $a$ = its alpha / 255 (float64, a trailing axis of 1); drawn on $v_s$ of a clip state with $g_s$ true: $f = H / $ `mouse_height` (float division), $x$ = `int(`$\ell_s$`["mouse"]["x"] * f)`, $y$ = `int(`$\ell_s$`["mouse"]["y"] * f)` (truncation toward zero); $h_c = \max(0, \min(H - y, k_c))$, $w_c = \max(0, \min(W - x, k_c))$: nothing drawn when $h_c = 0$ or $w_c = 0$; else the region $R$ = rows $y .. y + h_c - 1$, columns $x .. x + w_c - 1$ becomes `(R * (1 - a[:h_c, :w_c]) + S_rgb[:h_c, :w_c] * a[:h_c, :w_c]).astype(np.uint8)` (float64 arithmetic in this order, truncation).
(2) Meta text [U]: $\mu_s$ = `meta` with, in this order by `str.replace`, `{inventory}` → $\iota(\ell_s)$, `{hotbar}` → `str(`$\ell_s$`["hotbar"] + 1)`, `{yaw}` → $\mathrm{fmt}(\mathrm{wrap}(\ell_s[\text{yaw}]))$, `{pitch}` → $\mathrm{fmt}(\ell_s[\text{pitch}])$, `{x}` → $\mathrm{fmt}(\ell_s[\text{xpos}] - x_0)$, `{y}` → $\mathrm{fmt}(\ell_s[\text{ypos}])$, `{z}` → $\mathrm{fmt}(\ell_s[\text{zpos}] - z_0)$; $\iota(\ell)$ — (1) of `specs/vlm-labels.md` with this config's `inventory_line`, `item_line`, `no_items` (`no_items` for an empty inventory; else the entries grouped by `type`, quantities summed, one `item_line` per type in ascending Python string order, joined by `", "`); $\mathrm{fmt}(v)$ = `f"{v:.{decimals}f}"`, with its leading `-` removed when every digit of the result is 0; $\mathrm{wrap}(\psi) = ((\psi + 180) \bmod 360) - 180$ (Python float `%`) ([U]).
(3) Sequence of a stream [U]: state $s$ contributes the segment $\sigma_s = [b] + [p]^{N_s} + [e] + \tau_{tok}(\mu_s) + \tau_{tok}(\omega)$, $N_s = t h w / m_s^2$ of its row of `image_grid_thw` (4) (220 at 640×360); the sequence is $\sigma_0 \sigma_1 \dots$ in state order, nothing before $\sigma_0$ (no chat template, no BOS); $r_s$ — the position of the last token of $\sigma_s$ in the sequence (the readout).
(4) Image inputs [V1], [A], [U]: `Qwen2VLImageProcessor` (torchvision backend) built with `do_resize=True`, `size={"shortest_edge": min_pixels, "longest_edge": max_pixels}`, `resample=PILImageResampling.BICUBIC`, `do_rescale=True`, `rescale_factor`, `do_normalize=True`, `image_mean`, `image_std`, `do_convert_rgb=True`, `patch_size=`$P_v$, `temporal_patch_size=`$t_p$, `merge_size=`$m_s$, called on the frames $v_s$ in state order with `input_data_format="channels_last"`, `return_tensors="pt"` → `pixel_values` (the rows of all frames in order), `image_grid_thw` (one row per frame), on the CPU.
(5) Positions [V1], [A]: $\Pi$ = `B.get_rope_index(input_ids, mm_token_type_ids, image_grid_thw)` on the whole sequence (3) (`input_ids` of shape $(1, |\text{sequence}|)$, `mm_token_type_ids` = 1 exactly where the id is $p$, else 0, `int64`) — shape $(3, 1, |\text{sequence}|)$; always passed explicitly as `position_ids` ([U]).
(6) Hidden states of a clip: $h = B($`input_ids`, `pixel_values`, `image_grid_thw`, `position_ids` = $\Pi$, `use_cache=False`, no `attention_mask`$)$`.last_hidden_state` $\in \mathbb{R}^{|\text{sequence}| \times d_h}$, each clip in a forward of its own (batch 1), under autocast; $B$'s parameters float32 (loaded with `dtype=torch.float32`); gradient checkpointing on (`use_reentrant=False`).
(7) Head [U] (Q PER-6): $z = \mathrm{head}(h_r)$, $h_r$ cast to the dtype of the head's parameters (float32; float64 in the tests), outside autocast; $\mathrm{head}$ = `head_layers` blocks Linear → SiLU of width `head_width` (the first from $d_h$), then Linear to $D$ (unbounded); `head_layers` = 0 → one Linear $d_h \to D$; `torch.nn.Linear` with its default initialisation, drawn on the CPU inside `torch.random.fork_rng(devices=[])` after `torch.random.default_generator.manual_seed(seed)` ([U]: the head's initialisation does not depend on, and does not change, the state of any global generator, CPU or CUDA).
(8) $\phi$(clips) = $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times L \times D}$, in the dtype of the head: $Z_{i,s} = \mathrm{head}(h^{(i)}_{r_s})$ for clip $i$ by (3)–(7).
(9) Stream step [U] (R1): state $k$ of the stream ($k = 0, 1, \dots$; before state $k = L$ the stream restarts: empty cache and sequence, $k = 0$, $x_0, z_0$ from this state's line); its segment $\sigma_k$ (3) and its frame's `pixel_values` and `image_grid_thw` (4) go through $B$ under autocast with `use_cache=True`, the stream's cache (`DynamicCache(config=B.config)`, created at a (re)start) and `position_ids` = the columns of $\sigma_k$ in $\Pi$ of the stream's whole sequence so far (5); returns $z_k = \mathrm{head}(h_{r_k})$ (the head's dtype, shape $(D,)$), under `torch.no_grad`. Fed with a clip's frames $v_s$ (the cursor drawn) and lines, on the CPU with float64 parameters (untouched by autocast; the torch path of the delta rule), it gives the rows of $\phi$(clip) (8) up to the library's float32 rounding [V1]; elsewhere (float32 under bfloat16 autocast, FLA on the GPU) the chunking of the delta rule differs between the two and so do the results.
(10) Changed pair [U] (Q PER-8): $\chi_s = 1$ iff $\mu_s \ne \mu_{s+1}$ or $\frac{1}{3HW} \sum |v_{s+1} - v_s| > \tau$ (over all values of the frames with the cursor, as integers, the mean in float64), else 0; $s = 0..L-2$.
(11) Loss of clip $i$ [U], [S1]: with $z_s = Z_{i,s}$, $d_s = \lVert \alpha \odot (z_{s+1} - z_s) \rVert_2$ (`torch.linalg.vector_norm`, gradient 0 at 0 [V2]), $q_s = \min(\varepsilon, n_s^2 - d_s^2)$ (METRA's clamped `cst_penalty`):
$\ell^\phi_i = \frac{1}{L-1} \sum_{s=0}^{L-2} \Big[ \chi_s \big( -d_s - \mathrm{sg}(\lambda)\, q_s + \log\lambda \cdot \mathrm{sg}(q_s) \big) + \beta \big( \max(0, \lVert z_s \rVert_2^2 - c^2) + \max(0, \lVert z_{s+1} \rVert_2^2 - c^2) \big) \Big]$;
the gradient of $\log\lambda$ comes only from the last term of the bracket, $\phi$'s only from the others (METRA's two losses summed: [S1-code] minimises $-(\text{reward} + \mathrm{sg}(\lambda) q)$ for $\phi$ and $\log\lambda \cdot \mathrm{sg}(q)$ for $\lambda$); $\log\lambda$ starts at $\ln \lambda_0$; $\ell^\phi$ is computed in the dtype of $Z$ (float32; float64 in the tests).
(12) Groups [U], [T]: a parameter of $\phi$ is *decayed* iff its full name in `Phi.named_parameters()` is in `get_parameter_names(phi, [torch.nn.LayerNorm], P_T)`, $P_T$ the five patterns of [T]; $\phi$ declares, in this order, `phi_vision` / `phi_vision_no_decay` (the decayed / other parameters of `B.visual`), `phi_llm` / `phi_llm_no_decay` (of `B.language_model`), `phi_head` / `phi_head_no_decay` (of the head), each list in `named_parameters()` order; $\ell^\phi$ declares `phi_dual` = [$\log\lambda$]. The decay of each group is its config value (`specs/pretrain.md`).
(13) Latency ([U] 2026-10-20: every component on the per-step path states a budget; [U] 2026-10-22: soft): with $L$ = `states` of `configs/perception-data.yaml` ([U]), target — the median over $3L$ stream steps (9), after one warm-up stream of $L$ steps, each timed by `time.perf_counter` around the step and `torch.cuda.synchronize()`, at most 150 ms on the Spark's GPU with the real checkpoint and FLA, on synthetic states ([U]): frames 640×360 of uniform random `uint8` (a `numpy` generator seeded 0), lines with `hotbar` 0, `yaw` 0, `pitch` 0, position (0, 64, 0) and an inventory of 15 distinct item types of quantity 1 (named `item_0` … `item_14`); the `gpu` test (run by the user) fails only above 300 ms (one agent step) and prints the median and, for information, the time of one training forward and backward of a clip of $L$ such states.

## Domain
- Clips written by perception-data or preference-pool; on a state with $g_s$ true, `mouse.x`, `mouse.y` ≥ 0 ([V2]); the texts of the config other than `marker` and the item types of the lines contain no special-token string of $\tau_{tok}$ (e.g. `<|im_end|>`); every clip on the CPU, $\phi$ and $\ell^\phi$ on one device; $\phi$ in train mode in the loop, in eval mode for the stream; the environment's lines carry the fields of (2) and its frames are $H \times W \times 3$ RGB `uint8`.
- Kernels of the delta rule ([U], [K]): on the Spark (Linux aarch64) `fla-core` is installed, so `transformers` computes the gated delta rule by FLA's Triton kernels and $\phi$ runs there on CUDA only (a CPU forward fails with FLA); on other machines (this one: x86_64, no GPU) FLA is absent and the torch path runs; the tests that run the backbone on the CPU are skipped where `import fla.ops.gated_delta_rule` succeeds and gives `chunk_gated_delta_rule` (`transformers`' own condition, [K]) ([U]). `causal-conv1d` is absent everywhere (the torch convolution of M runs). FLA's environment variables (`FLA_*`, e.g. `FLA_CACHE_MODE`, `FLA_CONFIG_DIR`, `FLA_USE_TMA`) are unset: FLA tunes its kernels per process, so results on the GPU are not bitwise reproducible across runs ([U]; `specs/pretrain.md` promises identical reruns on the CPU only).
