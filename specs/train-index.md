## Notation
- $C$ — `clips_dir` (the `out_dir` of perception-data); $Q_c$ — `pair_clips_dir`, $Q_\ell$ — `pair_labels_dir` (Consumes), both set or both null (no pairs). The config's paths are resolved against the repository root `mcpref.REPO` wherever read or written; the messages written out in (R), (R') and the `out_dir` given to `check_fingerprints` show them as written; messages of reused helpers (`complete_shards`, `write_shard`) show resolved paths.
- $L$ — `config.states` of `{C}/run.json`; $W, H$ — its `config.width`, `config.height`.
- Unlabeled clips $U$: for each complete shard $k$ of `{C}/shards/` in order, each record of its `records.json` in order, each id of its `clips` (records whose `clips` is null skipped): the clip `{C}/shards/<name(k)>/clips/<id>`, its episode = the record's `relpath`.
- Pairs $R$: for each complete shard $k$ of `{Q_ℓ}/shards/` in order, each entry of its `pairs.json` in order: $(a, b, \ell)$; the clip of $a$ is `{Q_c}/shards/<name(a.shard)>/clips/<a.id>`, its episode `a.episode`; likewise $b$; $\ell$ — its `labels`.
- Episodes $e_0, e_1, \dots$ — in order of first appearance over $U$ in order, then over $R$ in order, $a$ before $b$.
- Groups $G_0, G_1, \dots$ — the connected components of the graph whose vertices are the episodes and whose edges are the pairs of $R$ (an edge between the episodes of $a$ and $b$; an episode without pairs is a group of its own), ordered by their first episode in the order of $e$.
- Item — `{"clip": p}` for a clip of $U$ (unlabeled), or `{"a": p_a, "b": p_b, "labels": ℓ}` for a pair of $R$ (labeled); $p$ — `str(PurePosixPath(<config path string>) / "shards" / name(k) / "clips" / id)`, relative to the repository root.
- $v$ — `validation`; $B$ — `items_per_shard`; $g$ = `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64([seed, 2]))`.
- $I_x$, $N_x$ — the items of part $x$ (0 = train, 1 = validation) in item order ($U$'s items, then $R$'s) and their count; $\sigma_x$ — (3); $h_x$ — the complete shards of part $x$.
- Clip-defining fields of a clips dataset's `run.json` (perception-data's format), named and ordered: `config.width`, `config.height`, `config.states`, `sha256.actions`, `sha256.clips`, `sha256.screens`, `sha256.gui_actions`, `sha256.overlay`, `sha256.overlay_screens`, `classifier`; `<names>` in a message — the Python `str` of the list of the names concerned in this order, e.g. `['config.width', 'sha256.actions']`.
- Config `configs/train-index.yaml` → `@dataclass(frozen=True) class TrainIndexConfig`, read with `config_mapping` (a missing or unknown key → `ValueError`): `clips_dir`, `pair_clips_dir`, `pair_labels_dir`, `out_dir` (strings, `pair_*` also null; each non-empty, relative, without a `..` component; `pair_clips_dir` and `pair_labels_dir` both null or both strings), `validation` (a string `float()` reads to $0 \le v < 1$, kept as written), `seed` (a YAML integer, not a boolean, $0 \le$ `seed` $< 2^{32}$), `items_per_shard` (a YAML integer, not a boolean, ≥ 1); violated constraints → `ValueError` at load.

## Math
(1) Split: the part of group $G_j$ = $g$`.choice(2, p=[1 - v, v])`, drawn for $j = 0, 1, \dots$ in order [N]; every episode of $G_j$ takes its part.
(2) Part of an item: the part of its episode (an unlabeled item) or of its two episodes, which lie in one group (a labeled item) — [U] on the audited draft.
(3) Shuffle, after every draw of (1): $\sigma_0$ = $g$`.permutation(N_0)`, then $\sigma_1$ = $g$`.permutation(N_1)` [N]; shard $s$ of part $x$ = the items $I_x[\sigma_x[i]]$, $i = sB .. \min((s+1)B, N_x) - 1$, in that order; $\lceil N_x / B \rceil$ shards.
(4) Fingerprints ([U] on the second audit), each the lowercase hex SHA-256 of a byte sequence: of a clips dataset $D$ ($C$, $Q_c$) — the UTF-8 bytes of one `json.dumps` of the dict {dotted name: value} over all its clip-defining fields in their order (default separators), then the bytes of the `records.json` of each complete shard of `{D}/shards/` in shard order; of $Q_\ell$ — the bytes of the `pairs.json` of each complete shard of `{Q_ℓ}/shards/` in shard order; of the config — the config file's bytes.

## Domain
- One run at a time; the reader (block pretrain) runs while no run does; no run of perception-data on $C$ or $Q_c$ or of the producer of $Q_\ell$ during a run.
- `{out_dir}` holds only what this block writes; clip ids unique within each clips dataset; the `pairs.json` of $Q_\ell$ well formed (Consumes), each referenced clip present in $Q_c$ with its `relpath` equal to the record's `episode` (not checked).

## Behaviour
(R) Checks before the inputs, in this order, each failing one aborting with a `RuntimeError` (paths as written in the config):
1. `{C}/run.json` exists and holds `clips` (`finished_run`, marker `clips`; `{C} is not complete`); with pairs, `{Q_c}/run.json` likewise (`{Q_c} is not complete`);
2. every clip-defining field present in `{C}/run.json` (`{C} lacks <names>`), then, with pairs, in `{Q_c}/run.json` (`{Q_c} lacks <names>`);
3. with pairs: the clip-defining fields of $Q_c$ equal $C$'s, each compared as JSON text (`{Q_c} differs from {C} in <names>`);
4. with pairs: `finished_run` of $Q_\ell$, marker `complete` (`{Q_ℓ} is not complete`).
(I) Inputs: the complete shards of `{C}/shards/`, then of `{Q_c}/shards/`, then of `{Q_ℓ}/shards/`, one shard at a time (bar `index inputs`): each `records.json` of $C$ (giving $U$) and of $Q_c$ and each `pairs.json` of $Q_\ell$ (giving $R$) fed to its dataset's fingerprint (4) as it is read; then the episodes, the groups and (1)–(3). The item lists are state, recomputed on every run (≈ 14 MB as JSON, ≈ 90 MB in memory at $1.5 \cdot 10^5$ items; the reading is assumed to end within a couple of seconds on real data, [U] 2026-10-20: the user returns to it if it does not).
(R') Checks after the inputs, in this order, each aborting with a `RuntimeError`: with pairs, `pair_clips` of `{Q_ℓ}/run.json` equals the fingerprint of $Q_c$ (`{Q_ℓ} labels other clips than {Q_c}`); `check_fingerprints` of an existing `{out_dir}/run.json`, in the order `config`, `clips`, `pair_clips`, `pair_labels` (null without pairs). Then `{out_dir}/run.json` = {`config` (the config file's mapping as written), `sha256`: {`config`, `clips`, `pair_clips`, `pair_labels`}, `states`: $L$, `width`: $W$, `height`: $H$} is written by `write_json`.
(W) For $x$ = 0 (`train`), then 1 (`validation`): shards $s = h_x .. \lceil N_x / B \rceil - 1$ of `{out_dir}/<train|validation>/`, each written by `write_shard` as `items.json` = `json.dumps({"items": [...]}, indent=2) + "\n"` (bar `index shards`). Then `{out_dir}/run.json` is rewritten by `write_json` with `"complete": true` and `counts` = {`train`: {`clips`, `pairs`, `episodes`}, `validation`: {`clips`, `pairs`, `episodes`}} added after it (`clips` — unlabeled items, `pairs` — labeled items, `episodes` — the episodes in the part). A part with $N_x = 0$ has no shard (its directory may be absent).
