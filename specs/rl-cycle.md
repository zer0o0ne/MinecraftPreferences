## Notation
- $c$ — one RL cycle and its trajectory shard index; $N$ — `steps` of `configs/env-sampling.yaml`; $H$ — PPO's horizon; $\tau_c$ — the fresh trajectory, continuous with the previous one; $\mathcal M_{c-1}$ — the incoming persistent History.
- $U^-=cN$ — the number of real actions before R1; $U=U^-+N$ — the number after R1. Fresh actions are $t\in[U^-,U)$ and R3 receives states $t\in[U^-,U]$.
- A fresh-transition item references one transition $t\to t+1$ of $\tau_c$. It always supplies factual-shift and METRA losses and supplies PPO loss iff $t+H\le U$.
- A pending item references an action of the incoming tail whose shift/METRA training has already happened and whose $H$ consequences have only now become complete. It supplies PPO loss only.
- A preference item is one fixed pair of $Q_c$ of `specs/rl-preference-pairs.md` and supplies preference loss only.
- $N_\Delta=N$; $N_{pend}$ — newly eligible incoming-tail positions; $N_A=N_{pend}+|\{t\in[U^-,U):t+H\le U\}|$; $R_c$ — the number of preference items; $I=N+N_{pend}+R_c$.
- $S$ — index shards; $b$ — `batch`; $b_\mu$ — `micro_batch`; $E$ — `epochs`; $M=\sum_s\lceil |I_s|/b\rceil$; $T=EM$ — optimizer steps of this cycle.
- $w_{ppo},w_\Delta,w_M,w_P$ — positive loss weights; $\ell^{ppo},\ell^\Delta,\ell^M$ from block 12, $\ell^P$ from block 5.
- $\phi,P,\pi$ without a cycle superscript are the current, changing modules inside training. All raw state references are re-encoded by the current $\phi$ on each use.
- $L$ — `states` of `configs/perception-data.yaml`; $Y_t,C_t$ — the last $L$ latent and preceding-action rows ending at state $t$; $z_t=Y_t[0,-1]$.
- Config `configs/rl-cycle.yaml` → frozen `RLCycleConfig`, fields in order: `sampling`, `ppo`, `rl_pairs`, `out_dir` (non-empty relative paths without `..`); `seed` (`integer` in $[0,2^{32}-1]$); `epochs`, `batch`, `micro_batch`, `items_per_shard`, `checkpoint_steps`, `keep_checkpoints` (`positive_int`); `w_ppo`, `w_delta`, `w_metra`, `w_P`, `adam_eps`, `grad_clip` (`number` > 0); `groups` (the pretrain group mapping); `betas` (two `number`s in $[0,1)$, stored as a tuple); `warmup_steps` (`integer` ≥ 0); `lr_floor` (`number` in $[0,1]$); `device` (`training.devices.device`). Missing/unknown keys and violated constraints raise `ValueError`.

## Math
(1) Initial order and mini-batch composition [U]: the source sequence is fresh items in ascending $t$, then pending items in ascending $t$, then $Q_c$ in shard/item order. Fresh/pending records are `{"kind":"fresh"|"pending","index":t}`; preference records are `{"kind":"preference","a":ref_dict,"b":ref_dict,"labels":labels}`. Apply one global permutation of $0..I-1$ drawn by `Generator(PCG64([seed,2])).permutation(I, axis=0)`. Store that permutation in `order` shards and resolve it into the self-contained mixed records above, at most `items_per_shard` per shard. Per epoch, `training.schedule` with `PCG64([seed,3])` shuffles shard order and then items within each shard; cut each permuted shard into consecutive mini-batches of $b$ items. Every item occurs once per epoch; a mini-batch never crosses a shard.

(2) One micro-batch [U]: read only its referenced raw data, encode all required real histories with the current $\phi$, and compute only the losses supplied by each item. PPO advantages are recomputed at this point by `specs/ppo.md` (1), (2), under stop-gradient. The factual target and METRA skill are stop-gradient. Preference labels and stored behaviour probabilities remain fixed. Process items in order with batch-1 policy and preference calls. A fresh eligible item has one `pi.readout(Y_t,C_t)[:, -1]` shared by its logits and predicted shift; its predicted shift feeds factual shift and detached METRA skill. A pending item computes only its logits and PPO advantage/loss; a fresh ineligible item computes no PPO call. Each micro-batch has one backward.

(3) Loss of mini-batch $\mathcal B$ [U], generalising `specs/pretrain.md` (1):
$\mathcal L(\mathcal B)=M\left[\frac{w_{ppo}}{N_A}\sum_{t\in A(\mathcal B)}\ell_t^{ppo}+\frac1{N_\Delta}\sum_{t\in D(\mathcal B)}(w_\Delta\ell_t^\Delta+w_M\ell_t^M)+\frac{w_P}{R_c}\sum_{r\in R(\mathcal B)}\ell_r^P\right]$,
where $A,D,R$ are the supplied PPO, fresh-transition and preference entries of $\mathcal B$; an empty sum is 0. The PPO term is absent when $N_A=0$, and the preference term is absent when $R_c=0$; neither is evaluated as $0/0$. The mean of $\mathcal L$ over the epoch's $M$ mini-batches equals the corresponding global mean over every supplied entry. Each loss's gradient reaches exactly the modules fixed by its own spec: PPO/shift → $\pi,\phi$; preference → $P,\phi$; METRA → $\phi$ and its dual variable, with predicted shift detached.

(4) Update [U]: cut $\mathcal B$ into consecutive micro-batches of $b_\mu$ items, call (2) and backward in order, accumulating gradients with the factors of (3). Gradients are set to `None` before the first micro-batch. After the last, apply pretrain's global gradient-norm clip and one AdamW step over all declared groups. There are no separate optimizer steps per network or loss. A parameter never reached by any backward keeps gradient `None` and is not updated. AdamW moments are local to one cycle; module weights and METRA's dual persist. The group order is `phi_vision`, `phi_vision_no_decay`, `phi_llm`, `phi_llm_no_decay`, `phi_head`, `phi_head_no_decay`, `metra_dual`, `pi`, `pi_no_decay`, `P`, `P_no_decay`. Pretrain (5) sets learning rates with $t=1..T$ independently in each cycle. $P$ and its groups remain present when $R_c=0$.

(5) Repeated epochs [U]: the same items occur once in each of $E$ epochs under a fresh schedule. Behaviour probabilities $\mu$, pair references and preference labels stay fixed; latents, PPO advantages and current action probabilities are recomputed on every occurrence. Pending items do not receive shift/METRA again.

(6) Restart-aware raw encoding [U]: for `RawHistory(states,actions,restart)` ending at $t$, split global state indices at every multiple of $L$. For each segment, construct each meta with that segment's first raw line as origin and call differentiable `Phi.frames_z` on the segment's RGB frames/metas. Concatenate segment latents chronologically. For every latent row, use `sampling.history.append` with the corresponding `RawHistory.actions` entry: the no-action row only for `None`, otherwise the one-hot of the action preceding that state. Truncate $Y,C$ together to the last $L$ rows. The executed action $a_t$ loaded from `StepRecord` is used only for PPO and the shift head; it is not assigned to row $t$'s preceding-action input.

## Behaviour
(C1) Cycle selection and R1: with no numeric cycle run, $c$ is the number of complete sampling trajectory shards. Otherwise, resume the highest numeric unfinished cycle run; if it is finalized, use its number + 1. Create the new cycle `run.json` before R1 with $c,U^-$, the initial-model identity and static fingerprints. Load cycle 0's `phi,pi,P` from pretrain `model.pt` and initialise `MetraLoss(PpoConfig)`; load all four stateful modules from cycle $c-1$'s `model.pt` for $c>0$. Construct a new `Sampler` with these supplied modules and the caller-owned live `Env`, call `reset()` once, then `sample()` once. No network training occurs while the environment advances. Exact real-world continuation requires that live `Env` at the corresponding world state; this block implements no simulator snapshot.

(C2) R3 data: construct `StateRef(f"{sampling.out_dir}#{c:06d}",t)` for $t=U^-..U$. Initialise pair RNG from `PCG64([seed,4])` for cycle 0 and from the previous cycle's pair `run.json.rng` thereafter. Before any update call `sample_pairs(pairs_cfg,c,states,encode_eval_start,P,alpha,pair_rng)`; its immutable $Q_c$ is fixed for the rest of the cycle. No History generation is published before training. A rerun of pair production continues from its own raw-work/RNG state.

(C3) Index: read the incoming tail (empty for cycle 0), select its $t+H\le U$ as pending items in ascending $t$, and verify continuity and uniqueness. Build (1)'s order and mixed-index shards. Write the remaining tail once as an explicit atomic shard, including an empty index list when its range is empty. An action enters PPO only when its horizon is complete and is not reintroduced afterwards; a fresh ineligible action still supplies shift/METRA.

(C4) Joint training: perform (1)–(6) with checkpoints every configured number of optimizer steps and at $T$. A fresh training start calls `torch.manual_seed(seed)` and creates new index/schedule generators from `[seed,2]`/`[seed,3]`; a checkpoint restores modules, optimizer, torch CPU/CUDA RNG and exact schedule position. Restoring step $T$ performs no more updates. Write final `model.pt` with CPU states under exactly `phi`, `pi`, `P`, `metra`; `PreferenceLoss` is stateless and reconstructed separately.

(C5) R5: after final `model.pt`, call `publish_memory(pairs_cfg,c,encode_eval_final)`. It re-encodes only selected seeds with final $\phi$, ages, cleans and publishes History. The next R1 constructs a new `Sampler` with the final model fingerprint, rebuilding perception streams while retaining stale MCTS coordinates. Save no second tail: C3's tail is already the next cycle's input.

(C6) Recovery: reconcile work from artifacts, not only the phase string. A complete trajectory shard $c$ satisfies sampling; pair phase `correction` or later satisfies pairs; complete order/index and tail satisfy indexing; existing final `model.pt` satisfies training; a complete published History generation $c$ satisfies finalisation. Skip satisfied phases, resume partial ones by their owners, and never repeat a published side effect. Update the cycle phase `sampled → pairs → indexed → trained → finalized` after each corresponding artifact; `complete=true` only at finalisation.

## Datasets and checkpoints
- `run.json`: `{cycle,u_before,phase,complete,config,sha256}`. Static fingerprints cover the RL config under key `rl_config`, the ppo/pair configs and the ordered env-sampling config identities (`config`, `pretrain_config`, `env`, `mcts`, `perception_data`, `perception`, `action_mask`, `policy`, `preference`, `engine`, `classifier`, `clips`, `vpt_actions`, `gui_screens`) plus `initial_model` = SHA-256 of the source pretrain/previous-cycle `model.pt`. Later keys are `trajectory`, `incoming_tail`, `pairs` (ordered `items.json` contents, captured before publication changes its run), `order`, `index`, `tail` (ordered shard contents). The trajectory digest concatenates each lexicographically ordered relative POSIX path encoded as UTF-8, one NUL byte, then that file's bytes. Never fingerprint a mutable producer `run.json` as input identity.
- `order/shards/<name(k)>/indices.json`: `{"indices":[global source positions]}`, at most `items_per_shard`; `order/run.json` records the post-shuffle RNG and content fingerprint. `index/shards/<name(k)>/items.json`: `{"items":[self-contained records of (1)]}` in the same order. `index/run.json`: `{complete:true,counts:{fresh:N,pending:N_pend,ppo:N_A,preference:R_c,items:I},batches:M,steps:T,sha256:...}`.
- `tail/shards/000000/items.json`: `{"next_step":U,"indices":list(range(max(0,U-H+1),U))}`, including an empty list. The previous cycle's complete tail is the only pending input; its content is fingerprinted before indexing.
- `checkpoints/<name(j)>/checkpoint.pt`: `{step,epoch,schedule_rng,batch_in_epoch,modules,optimizer,torch_rng,cuda_rng}` with pretrain's position semantics and CPU module states; `modules` contains `phi,pi,P,metra`. Written when `checkpoint_steps` divides $t$ and always at $t=T$; a rerun continues after the last complete shard and gives the same CPU result as uninterrupted training. `losses.json` retains every step since the previous checkpoint: `{step,ppo,shift,metra,P,loss,lr}`; `lr` maps every group to its current rate. Every losses file is retained; only old `checkpoint.pt` files are pruned to `keep_checkpoints`.
- `model.pt`: CPU `state_dict()` mapping `phi,pi,P,metra`, atomically published after training. For R5 and the next cycle it supersedes the initial/checkpoint weights.

## Progress bars
- `rl cycle` (unit `step`) on stderr: total $T$, restored initial position, advanced after each optimizer update; postfix `{ppo,shift,metra,P,loss}` using the current step's logged values. Restoring step $T$ prints `rl cycle: already done` instead of drawing a bar.
- `rl index` (unit `item`) on stderr: total the shuffle exchanges and mixed-item resolutions, restored initial from the completed work, advanced per exchange/resolution. A complete earlier index prints `rl index: already done`. Blocks 9 and 32 retain their own bars.
