## Sources
- [U] the user, 2026-10-22: $P$ takes two latent states, each with up to $L_P - 1$ previous latents; a transformer whose layers are of two kinds — S, through which each history passes on its own with the same weights, and X, cross-attention of each history on the other; a linear head with sigmoids giving $p_\succ$ and $p_{inc}$; no causality inside the transformer; in pretraining the history of a compared state is the latents of the preceding states of its clip, computed in the same pass of $\phi$ ($L_P \le L$, so the history is always full there; at RL a history is short only in the first $L_P - 1$ steps of the timeline, and a history crossing a restart of $\phi$'s stream is accepted, any remedy left to blocks 2 and 8); the agent's memory of past latents belongs to other blocks (8, 9, 11); positions by RoPE; S attention bidirectional; X layer: each history attends to the other from the layer's inputs, the same weights in both directions, then an MLP per history; the layer order a config list, starting with S, X, S, X.

## Notation
- $D$ — the latent size of $\phi$, given by the caller (`latent`).
- History — $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times \ell \times D}$, rows oldest first, row $\ell - 1$ the compared state; $m \ge 1$ pairs, $\ell \ge 1$; in pretraining $Y_a = Z_{i_a(R)}$, $Y_b = Z_{i_b(R)}$ of `specs/pretrain.md`, $\ell = L$; at RL given by the caller. In one call each argument has one length: $\ell_a$, $\ell_b$, possibly different.
- $L_P$ — `history`; per argument $n = \min(L_P, \ell)$ — its used rows $Y_{:, \ell - n : \ell}$; the position of used row $j = 0..n-1$: $\varpi_j = j - (n - 1)$ (the compared state at 0, both arguments aligned there).
- $d$ — `width`; $h$ — `heads`, heads $\eta = 1..h$, $d_h = d / h$ (even); $f$ — `hidden`; $\vartheta$ — `rope_base`; $\epsilon$ — `norm_eps`; $\Lambda$ — `layers`, a list of `"S"` / `"X"`; $\varsigma$ — `seed`.
- Parameters, all float32 (float64 in the tests): $W_{in} \in \mathbb{R}^{D \times d}$; per layer $W_q, W_k, W_v, W_o \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$, $W_1, W_3 \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times f}$, $W_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{f \times d}$, the gains $\gamma_1, \gamma_2 \in \mathbb{R}^d$; the final gain $\gamma_f$; the head $w_\succ, w_{inc} \in \mathbb{R}^d$, $b_{inc} \in \mathbb{R}$. A matrix acts on row vectors: $x W$.
- $\mathrm{N}_\gamma(x) = x / \sqrt{\mathrm{mean}(x^2) + \epsilon} \odot \gamma$ — RMSNorm over the last axis [S2].
- $H^0_a$, $H^0_b$ — the tokens (1); $A$, $B$ — the two arguments' states between layers; $r_a$, $r_b$ — the readouts (7); $(s, t)$ — the logits (8).
- $\sigma$ — the logistic sigmoid; $\mathrm{BCE}(x, y) = -\left(y \log \sigma(x) + (1 - y) \log \sigma(-x)\right)$ on a logit $x$ and a target $y \in [0, 1]$.
- Config `configs/preference.yaml` → `@dataclass(frozen=True) class PreferenceConfig`, fields in this order, read with `config_mapping` (a missing or unknown key → `ValueError`), violated constraints → `ValueError` at load: `history`, `width`, `heads`, `hidden` (`positive_int`; `width` divisible by `heads` with an even quotient); `layers` (a non-empty list of the strings `"S"`, `"X"`, stored as a tuple); `rope_base`, `norm_eps` (`number`, > 0); `seed` (`integer` in $[0, 2^{32} - 1]$); `latency_ms`, `latency_fail_ms` (`number`, > 0); `latency_calls` (`positive_int`); `positive_int`, `number`, `integer` of `mcpref`.

## Math
(1) Tokens, per argument: $H^0 = Y_{:, \ell - n : \ell} W_{in}$.
(2) RoPE [S1], [U]: for $x \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h}$ at position $\varpi$, $i = 0..d_h/2 - 1$, $\theta_i = \vartheta^{-2i/d_h}$: $\mathrm{R}(x, \varpi)_i = x_i \cos(\varpi \theta_i) - x_{i + d_h/2} \sin(\varpi \theta_i)$, $\mathrm{R}(x, \varpi)_{i + d_h/2} = x_{i + d_h/2} \cos(\varpi \theta_i) + x_i \sin(\varpi \theta_i)$; $\theta_i$ and the angles computed in each call, in the dtype of $x$ (that of the parameters within the Domain).
(3) Attention of queries $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n_q \times d}$ (positions $\varpi^q$) on keys $K \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n_k \times d}$ (positions $\varpi^k$): per pair and head $\eta$ (columns $(\eta - 1) d_h .. \eta d_h - 1$), $q_j = \mathrm{R}((Q W_q)_{j, \eta}, \varpi^q_j)$, $\kappa_{j'} = \mathrm{R}((K W_k)_{j', \eta}, \varpi^k_{j'})$, $v_{j'} = (K W_v)_{j', \eta}$; $o_{j, \eta} = \sum_{j' = 0}^{n_k - 1} \mathrm{softmax}_{j'}\left(q_j \cdot \kappa_{j'} / \sqrt{d_h}\right) v_{j'}$ (no mask, no causality, [U]); $\mathrm{Attn}(Q, K) = [o_{\cdot, 1}, \dots, o_{\cdot, h}]\, W_o$.
(4) MLP [S3]: $\mathrm{F}(x) = \left(\mathrm{SiLU}(x W_1) \odot x W_3\right) W_2$.
(5) Layer S, on each argument $H$ alone, the same parameters for both: $H \leftarrow H + \mathrm{Attn}(\mathrm{N}_{\gamma_1}(H), \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_1}(H))$ (positions $\varpi$ of the argument on both sides), then $H \leftarrow H + \mathrm{F}(\mathrm{N}_{\gamma_2}(H))$.
(6) Layer X, from the layer's inputs $A$, $B$: $\hat A = \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_1}(A)$, $\hat B = \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_1}(B)$; $A' = A + \mathrm{Attn}(\hat A, \hat B)$, $B' = B + \mathrm{Attn}(\hat B, \hat A)$ (one set of parameters; the queries' positions those of their argument, the keys' those of the other); then $A'' = A' + \mathrm{F}(\mathrm{N}_{\gamma_2}(A'))$, $B'' = B' + \mathrm{F}(\mathrm{N}_{\gamma_2}(B'))$.
(7) Stack: $(A, B) = (H^0_a, H^0_b)$ passed through the layers of $\Lambda$ in order, each with its own parameters; $r_a = \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_f}(A_{:, n_a - 1})$, $r_b = \mathrm{N}_{\gamma_f}(B_{:, n_b - 1})$ (the compared states' rows).
(8) Head [U]: $s = (r_a - r_b) \cdot w_\succ$, $t = (r_a + r_b) \cdot w_{inc} + b_{inc}$; $p_\succ = \sigma(s)$, $p_{inc} = \sigma(t)$; $P$ returns $(s, t)$, each of shape $(m,)$.
(9) Swap: (1)–(7) treat the two arguments alike, so $P(Y_b, Y_a) = (-s, t)$: $p_\succ(b, a) = 1 - p_\succ(a, b)$, $p_{inc}(b, a) = p_{inc}(a, b)$, exactly in the math.
(10) Loss of pair $r$ with $(s_r, t_r)$ and labels $\{p, \text{inc}\}$: the contract of `specs/acyclic.md` (5), $\mathrm{BCE}(t_r, \text{inc}) + \mathrm{BCE}(t_r, \text{inc}) + [\text{inc} = 0]\left(\mathrm{BCE}(s_r, p) + \mathrm{BCE}(-s_r, 1 - p)\right)$, equals by (9) and $\mathrm{BCE}(-x, 1 - y) = \mathrm{BCE}(x, y)$: $\ell^P_r = 2\,\mathrm{BCE}(t_r, \text{inc}) + 2\,[\text{inc} = 0]\,\mathrm{BCE}(s_r, p)$ — computed so, from the one call of $P$ by the loop ([U]); in the dtype of $s$, $t$, the targets built on their device and in their dtype; the term of $s_r$ for `inc` = 1 removed by `torch.where` with the finite placeholder target 0.5.
(11) Initialisation [U]: $P$ built on the CPU inside `torch.random.fork_rng(devices=[])` after `torch.random.default_generator.manual_seed(`$\varsigma$`)` (as $\phi$'s head, `specs/perception.md` (7)); the submodules created in the attribute order of the Interface; torch's defaults: every `Linear` $\mathcal{U}(\pm 1/\sqrt{\text{fan\_in}})$ [T] ($w_\succ$ the weight of a `Linear(d, 1, bias=False)`, $w_{inc}$, $b_{inc}$ the weight and bias of a `Linear(d, 1)`), every gain 1; no dropout. The caller moves $P$ to its device.
(12) Groups [U], the HF Trainer's rule of `specs/perception.md` (12) (no decay on biases and norm weights), which for $P$'s parameters reads: $P$ declares, in this order, `P` — the weights of its `Linear`s ($W_{in}$, every $W_q, W_k, W_v, W_o, W_1, W_3, W_2$, $w_\succ$, $w_{inc}$) and `P_no_decay` — the gains $\gamma$ and $b_{inc}$, each list in `named_parameters()` order; $\ell^P$ declares none.
(13) Latency [U] (soft, as `specs/perception.md` (13)): target — the median over `latency_calls` calls with $m = 1$, $\ell_a = \ell_b = L_P$, after `latency_calls` warm-up calls, at most `latency_ms` on the Spark's GPU; $P$ in eval mode, float32, under `torch.no_grad`; after `torch.manual_seed(0)` the inputs $Y_a$, then $Y_b$, each `torch.randn(1, L_P, D, device=<the GPU>)`, made before the warm-up, $D$ = `len(alpha)` of `configs/perception.yaml`; each call timed by `time.perf_counter` with `torch.cuda.synchronize()` before starting and before stopping the timer; the `gpu` test (run by the user) prints the median and fails only above `latency_fail_ms`.
