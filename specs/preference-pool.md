## Notation
- $P$ — the perception-data config named by `perception` (`PerceptionDataConfig`); its access fields, `ffmpeg_timeout`, `clips`, `screens`, `gui_actions`, `overlay`, `classifier_config`, `states` ($L$), `classifier_batch` are used here; its `per_episode`, `n_clips`, `seed`, `workers`, `out_dir` are not.
- Exact value of a YAML number $x$ of this config: `fractions.Fraction(str(x))` of the number YAML reads, by `exact` of `mcpref` (the shortest repr of the float read, equal to the decimal as written when it has at most 15 significant digits; [U]).
- Entries: for each subset of $P$'s `index_urls` in config order, its initial pool (perception-data Notation), without the relpaths whose version is below `min_version` (compared as in perception-data (1)); an entry's name — `PurePosixPath(relpath).stem`, split from the right into `<alias>-<hex>-<date>-<time>`; its URL stem — the index's `basedir` + its relpath without `.mp4` (perception-data).
- Group: the entries with the same (subset, alias, hex); groups in order of their first entry (subsets in config order, pools in order); a group's entries in pool order.
- Head of an entry: (1); its status; for `ok`, its fields $\sigma$ (`stats`), $t$ (`serverTick`), $\mu$ (`milli`).
- $B$ — `head_bytes`; $\delta_t$ — `tick_ms`; $\varepsilon$ — the exact `tick_slack`; $Z$ — `resetting_stats` (stats keys that may decrease within a session).
- Head shard $\eta = 0, 1, \dots$ — consecutive groups, a new head shard started after the group that brings the current one to $\ge$ `entries_per_shard` entries; $h_H$ — the complete head shards.
- Session — (S); its pieces in order; its subset; its key — {`subset`, `start`: the relpath of its first piece}.
- $g$ = `Generator(PCG64([seed, 3]))` — the plan's generator; draw $k$ = one session; $e$ — `episodes`; $m_p$ — `per_episode`; $N_p$ — `n_clips`; $h$ — the complete shards of `{out_dir}/shards/`; $c$ — the clips in them; $n_e$, $n_s$, $n_c$ — the records, the shards and the comparisons in them.
- Clips of draw $k$: those of its episodes in episode order, each episode's in the order taken; $n$ — their count; numbered $0..n-1$.
- $r$ — `partners`; $\rho$ — the exact `cycles`; $g_k$ = `Generator(PCG64([seed, 5, k]))` — the graph generator of draw $k$; $E$ — the edge set of (G).
- JSON files of this block: `json.dumps(value, indent=2) + "\n"`, read with `json.loads`.

## Math
(1) Head of an entry: one GET of URL stem + `.jsonl` with the headers `Range: bytes=0-{B-1}` and `x-ms-version` (a transport error or HTTP 5xx retried after `retry_delay` up to `http_retries` times, by `mcpref.http.retry.request`): 404 → `missing`; 416 → `empty`; 206 → its body, replaced by the whole file from the ranged fetch (f), on a client of its own (default limits, header `x-ms-version`), when the body holds no `\n` and the size of `Content-Range` exceeds $B$ (the size: group 3 of `re.fullmatch(r"bytes (\d+)-(\d+)/(\d+)", <header>)`; a missing header or no match then → `RuntimeError(f"GET {url}: HTTP 206 without Content-Range")`; the fetch's `None` → `missing`, `b""` → `empty`); any other status → `RuntimeError(f"GET {url}: HTTP {status}")`. Line 0 — the bytes before the first `\n` (all of them when there is none); `json.loads` of line 0 raising `ValueError` (invalid UTF-8 included), or not an object whose `stats` is an object and whose `serverTick` and `milli` have `type(x) is int` → `malformed`; else `ok`.
(S) Sessions of a group ([U], [R]): its `ok` entries sorted by $(\mu, \text{relpath})$, in one pass holding the current chain (none at the start): an entry with empty $\sigma$ starts a new chain, a session; an entry $j$ with non-empty $\sigma$ joins the current chain, whose last piece is $i$, iff $d = t_j - t_i > 0$, $\delta_t\, d\, (1 - \varepsilon) \le \mu_j - \mu_i$ (exact), and every key of $\sigma_i$ not in $Z$ is in $\sigma_j$ with a value $\ge$ its value in $\sigma_i$; otherwise (or without a current chain) $j$ starts a new chain, an orphan chain. The orphan chains are dropped; the sessions are the other chains in the order they were started, each with its pieces in order.
(2) Draw $k$, until $c \ge N_p$ or no subset is active (a subset is active while its pool of sessions is non-empty; the pools in the order of `sessions/`): subset = the active subsets in config order at $g$`.integers(len(active))`; session = `pool.pop(`$g$`.integers(len(pool)))`; its episodes: all its pieces if they are at most $e$, else the pieces at the positions `sorted(`$g$`.permutation(len(pieces))[:e])`; in piece order.
(3) Episode $j$ (0-based, in the order of (2)) of draw $k$: the checks of perception-data (1) except `version` (`missing`, `empty`, `malformed`, `options`); a candidate is processed by perception-data (2)–(5) with $m_p$ instead of `per_episode`, the generator `Generator(PCG64([seed, 4, k, j]))` instead of `[seed, 1, k]`, its video at `staging/<k>/<j>.mp4` and its clips at `staging/<k>/<id>.part/`.
(G) Comparisons of draw $k$ with $n \ge 2$ ([U]), with $g_k$:
  1. for $i = 0..n-1$: $O_i$ = $0..n-1$ without $i$, ascending; the edges $\{i, O_i[q]\}$ for $q$ in $g_k$`.permutation(n - 1)[:min(r, n - 1)]`, added to the set $E$ of unordered pairs;
  2. while the graph $(0..n-1, E)$ has more than one connected component: the components ordered by their smallest vertex, each one's vertices ascending; $q_0, q_1$ = $g_k$`.permutation(<number of components>)[:2]`; $u$ = component $q_0$`[`$g_k$`.integers(<its size>)]`, then $v$ = component $q_1$`[`$g_k$`.integers(<its size>)]`; $\{u, v\}$ added;
  3. while $|E| - n + 1 < \lceil \rho n \rceil$ (exact) and $|E| < n(n-1)/2$: the pairs $(i, j)$, $i < j$, not in $E$, in lexicographic order, the one at $g_k$`.integers(<their count>)` added;
  4. the pairs of $E$ in lexicographic order of $(i, j)$, $i < j$; for each, $o$ = $g_k$`.integers(2)`: the comparison $(a, b) = (i, j)$ if $o = 0$, else $(j, i)$.
  $n < 2$: no comparison, no clip written.

## Domain
- `{out_dir}` holds only what this block writes; one run at a time; the readers run while no run does; the Domain of perception-data for $P$'s inputs.
- Every name matches `<alias>-<hex>-<date>-<time>`; the values of `stats` are JSON numbers ([R]); not checked.

## Behaviour
(0) Indices as perception-data (0), into `{out_dir}/indices/`.
(H) Heads: for $\eta = h_H, \dots$: the heads of head shard $\eta$'s entries fetched on `head_workers` threads sharing the `httpx.Client` of (0) (header `x-ms-version`, default limits; at most 20 requests at once on it, the fallback of (1) using a client of its own); if any raises, queued fetches are cancelled, running ones finish, and the exception of the failing entry first in the head shard's order (its groups in order, each group's entries in pool order) is raised; once all are fetched, (S) is applied to each of its groups and head shard $\eta$ is written by `write_shard` (Datasets); bar `sessions`.
(P) Plan: the sessions of all head shards read one at a time (state, recomputed on every run: ≈ $1.1 \cdot 10^5$ relpaths); continuation as perception-data (7) (bar `resume`): the $h$ complete shards read one at a time, accumulating $n_e$, $n_s = h$, $n_c$ (from each `comparisons.json`) and the reasons of the records; each pool without the sessions whose keys are in their `state.json`, its order kept; $g$ set to the `generator` of shard $h - 1$ (`Generator(PCG64([seed, 3]))` when $h = 0$), $c$ to its `clips`.
(W) Shards: the planner makes the draws of (2) in order and submits each draw's episodes, in order, to a pool of `workers` threads, each episode one work item running (3) (the checks, then the processing of a candidate) with an `httpx.Client` of its own (header `x-ms-version`, default limits) for its options file, `.jsonl` and video; it starts draw $k'$ only while fewer than `workers` episodes of draws before $k'$ are not yet written. Shard $k$ is written by `write_shard` once all episodes of draw $k$ are done and shard $k - 1$ is written, with `records.json` (one record per episode of (2), in order, in perception-data's record format, `clips` = the ids of the episode's clips written to this shard for a valid episode, `[]` for a valid episode when $n < 2$), `state.json` = {`generator`: $g$'s state at the end of draw $k$, before draw $k + 1$'s first `integers`, `clips`: $c$ after shard $k$, `session`: its key}, `options/<version>_<stem>.json` (as perception-data (6)), `clips/<id>/` (all $n$ clips renamed from staging, none when $n < 2$; perception-data's clip format), `comparisons.json` = a list, per comparison of (G) in order, of {`a`: {`id`, `episode`}, `b`: likewise} (`episode` = the clip's `relpath`; `[]` when $n < 2$); then `staging/<k>/` is removed. After the shard that brings $c \ge N_p$ (the session written whole; $c$ may exceed $N_p$, [U]): queued work is cancelled, running work finishes with its results and exceptions ignored, then `staging/` is removed. The outputs do not depend on `workers` or timing.
(8') Rerun and `run.json` as perception-data (8) — the check and the first write of `run.json` before (0) —, with: `config` = this block's config mapping as written (keys in `PreferencePoolConfig` order), followed by $P$'s output fields of Notation as written in $P$'s file; the check over the output fields of Notation (`config.<name>` in the message, in the order of Notation), then `sha256.<key>`, then `classifier`; `staging/` removed after the check; the counts added at the top level at the end of a run that completes, as there: `episodes` (reasons over the records of all shards), `sessions` ($n_s$), `clips` ($c$, the marker), `comparisons` ($n_c$).
(9') Failures as perception-data (9): a worker's exception is raised when draw order reaches its draw (the earlier shards written first), the first failing episode in episode order when several of the draw fail; the message printed by `main` of `pool/run.py`.
