## Notation
- Classifier $\gamma$: a callable mapping a non-empty list of RGB frames ($H \times W \times 3$, `uint8`, the view (5) of `specs/vpt-gui-frames.md`) to a list of class names of $\mathcal{K} \cup \{\text{other}\}$, one per frame; assumed deterministic per frame (a frame's class does not depend on the rest of its batch); $\gamma_{id}$ — a string naming it, recorded in `run.json`.
- Episode $e$ drawn at position $r = 0, 1, \dots$ of the draw order; its `.jsonl` bytes $J_e$; its lines $\ell_0..\ell_{T-1}$ — the pieces of $J_e$ split on `\n` (a final `\n` ends the last line), each parsed as JSON; its frames $0..T$.
- $N$, $m$, $L$ — `n_clips`, `per_episode`, `states`.
- Config `configs/perception-data.yaml` → `@dataclass(frozen=True) class PerceptionDataConfig`; integers are YAML integers (not booleans), durations YAML integers or floats; violated constraints → `ValueError` at load:
  - access fields of `GuiFramesConfig`, same meaning: `index_urls`, `x_ms_version`, `min_version`, `required_options`, `width`, `height`, `fetch_streams` (1–20), `http_timeout`, `http_retries`, `retry_delay`, `ffmpeg`;
  - `ffmpeg_timeout` (s, one whole-episode decode);
  - `clips`, `screens`, `gui_actions`, `overlay` — paths of `configs/clips.yaml`, `configs/gui-screens.yaml`, `configs/vpt-gui-actions.yaml`, `configs/f3-overlay.yaml`; all paths relative to the repository root, resolved against it;
  - `states` ($L \ge 2$), `per_episode` ($m \ge 1$), `n_clips` ($N \ge 1$), `seed` ($0 \le$ seed $< 2^{32}$), `workers` (≥ 1), `classifier_batch` (≥ 1), `out_dir`.

## Behaviour
(1) Plan: one `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64(seed))` draws subsets and episodes as `specs/vpt-gui-frames.md` (3) does (`integers` for the subset and for the pool position, the pool popped), until $N$ clips are committed or every pool is empty; the reason of an episode is (2) of that spec in its order without `no_gui`: `version`, `missing`, `empty`, `malformed`, `options`; then `video` and `no_clip` below. A valid episode is processed by (2)–(5) with its own generator `Generator(PCG64([seed, 1, r]))`.
(2) Video: the `.mp4` by the ranged fetch (f) (a 404 → reason `video`) into `{out_dir}/staging/<r>.mp4`; pass 1 decodes it with `[ffmpeg, "-nostdin", "-loglevel", "error", "-i", <video>, "-fps_mode", "passthrough", "-f", "rawvideo", "-pix_fmt", "yuv420p", "pipe:1"]`; a decode that does not end within `ffmpeg_timeout` with exit code 0 and exactly $T + 1$ frames is retried after `retry_delay` up to `http_retries` times, then gives the reason `video`. Pass 1 keeps frame 0 and every frame $t \le T - 1$ with `isGuiOpen` of $\ell_t$ true.
(3) Classes: every frame is classified at most once per episode, its class kept for the rest of the episode; the frames of `needed_frames` are classified in their order, in consecutive chunks of `classifier_batch` frames, the last one shorter; `gui_actions` (with `double_click_ms`) gives the GUI segments, $C$ and the GUI foreign lines. Calls of $\gamma$ are serialised across workers.
(4) Modes: the start is (False, False, 0) if `starts_session`, else (`overlay_shown` on `luma` of frame 0, False, 0).
(5) Clips: `evidence`, `kept`, `valid_starts`, then `sample` with $L$, $m$, the episode's generator and `accept` = the not yet classified frames of the clip's states with the GUI flag true are classified (in state order, in consecutive chunks of `classifier_batch` frames, the last one shorter), and no state is in `excluded_states`; no clip → reason `no_clip`. Pass 2 decodes the video again with the same command and keeps the frames of the taken clips' states (a failure aborts the run); each clip is written to the directory `{out_dir}/staging/<r>/<id>.part/`; the video is deleted in every case.
(6) Commit, in draw order, in this order: the options file is written; the episode's first $N - c$ clips in the order taken ($c$ the clips already committed; all when fewer) are renamed from staging to `clips/<id>` and appended to `clips.jsonl`; then the episode's record is appended to `episodes.jsonl` (its `clips` lists the committed ids); then its staging is removed; files are flushed before the next commit. An episode counts as committed once its record is written. Up to `workers` valid episodes are processed at once, ahead of the one to commit; work done for episodes after the one that reaches $N$ is discarded and its staging removed. The outputs do not depend on `workers` or timing.
(7) Rerun: `run.json` holds the config, the index ETags, the SHA-256 of `configs/vpt-actions.yaml` (as named by the clips config), of the four config files named above and of the catalogue named by the overlay config, and $\gamma_{id}$; it is written before planning; a rerun with any of them different aborts. Otherwise the plan is replayed from `episodes.jsonl` (the draws repeated with the recorded reasons, nothing fetched), the committed episodes and their clips are kept, the lines of `clips.jsonl` and the directories of `clips/` that no record lists are removed, `staging` is emptied, and planning continues after the last committed episode.
(8) Failures: HTTP after the retries aborts the run (as (f)); an exception of $\gamma$, of pass 2 or of storage aborts the run: the queued episodes are cancelled, the running ones finish, the bar closes, and `collect` re-raises the exception; the one-line message, the last line of stderr, is printed by `main` (wired with the classifier, as in `specs/vpt-gui-frames.md`). ffmpeg's stderr is printed with `tqdm.write` as in `specs/vpt-gui-frames.md`.

## Interface
- Modules in `src/mcpref/clips/` (with block clips): `collect.py` (`PerceptionDataConfig`, `load_config`, `def collect(cfg: PerceptionDataConfig, classify: Callable[[list[np.ndarray]], list[str]], classifier_id: str) -> None` — (1), (6)–(8)), `episode.py` ((2)–(5) for one episode), `store.py` (records, files, staging, replay).
- Reuse of `src/mcpref/vpt/` (block vpt-gui-frames, outputs and tests unchanged): the prototype extracts what both blocks need — the access fields as a `typing.Protocol` that both config dataclasses satisfy (the reused functions take it instead of `GuiFramesConfig`), the episode draw without the frame part, the reasons without `no_gui` (`no_gui` stays in block vpt-gui-frames), the raw bytes of the fetched `.jsonl`, `index`, `download_indices`, `fetch`, `check_rerun`, `write_run` — and adds the whole-episode decode of (2).
- `main` and `scripts/collect_perception_pretrain_data.sh` (texture download of `specs/gui-screens.md` first, then `main`) are added by the cycle that plugs in the classifier ([U]).
- Each episode's video is fetched with its own `httpx.Client` (default limits; the planner has its own client for the indices and episode files).
- Consumes: the indices and episode files of VPT; `font/ascii.png` under `textures.out_dir` of the overlay's catalogue; the configs above.
- Produces `{out_dir}/`:
  - `run.json`: `{"config": <the config file's mapping as written, paths relative>, "index_etags": {…}, "sha256": {"actions", "clips", "screens", "gui_actions", "overlay", "overlay_screens"}, "classifier": γ_id, "episodes": {reason or "null": count}, "clips": count}` (counts written at the end of a run that completes);
  - `episodes.jsonl` (draw order, committed episodes only): `subset`, `relpath`, `reason` (string or `null`), and, `null` unless computed: `options` (object; for the reasons `options`, `video`, `no_clip` and valid), `starts_session` (bool, computed from the lines before the `.mp4` is fetched; for `video`, `no_clip` and valid), `overlay_start` (bool; `false` when the episode starts a session; for `no_clip` and valid), `valid_starts` (count; for `no_clip` and valid), `clips` (ids; for valid);
  - `options/<version>_<stem>-options.json` — the options file of every committed episode with a reason after `empty`, byte-identical;
  - `clips.jsonl` (commit order): `id` = `<version>_<stem>_<first state line>`, `subset`, `relpath`, `version`, `states` (lines), `actions` (per interval, list of names), `n` (per interval), `gui` (per state), `classes` (per state: the class, `null` for a state whose GUI flag is false), `pitch` (per state);
  - `clips/<id>/frames.yuv` — the $L$ state frames, raw yuv420p, concatenated in state order; `clips/<id>/lines.jsonl` — the lines of the clip's span as in $J_e$, each with its `\n`.
- Progress bars (AGENTS.md Code): `indices` (unit `file`) as in vpt-gui-frames; `collect` (unit `clip`): total $N$, initial value the clips already committed; advanced at each commit by the clips committed; postfix `<k> episodes` (committed episodes), set at creation and after every commit; ends below $N$ when the pools run dry; the work inside an episode (fetches, decodes, classification) draws no bar of its own ([U] 2026-10-15: an exception to AGENTS.md Progress, like (f) of vpt-gui-frames); on a rerun with nothing left to do ($N$ already committed, or every pool exhausted by the committed draws) → the line `collect: already done` instead of the bar.
- Starting config values: the access fields as in `configs/vpt-gui-frames.yaml`, `required_options` extended with `autoJump`: false, `toggleCrouch`: false, `touchscreen`: false and the key bindings of `specs/vpt-actions.md` and `specs/vpt-gui-actions.md` (Domain); `ffmpeg_timeout` = 600, `clips` = `configs/clips.yaml`, `screens` = `configs/gui-screens.yaml`, `gui_actions` = `configs/vpt-gui-actions.yaml`, `overlay` = `configs/f3-overlay.yaml`, `states` = 16, `per_episode` = 10, `n_clips` = 10000, `seed` = 0, `workers` = 4, `classifier_batch` = 64, `out_dir` = `data/perception`. Output size at these values: ≈ 55 GB of frames plus 6–18 GB of lines.
- Tests: with a fake HTTP server, a fake `ffmpeg` (raw frames of known content, failures, a wrong frame count) and a fake classifier (counting its calls): the plan and every reason (`video` for a missing `.mp4` and for a failed decode, `no_clip`); classification at most once per frame, batches, no empty call; identical outputs for `workers` 1 and 3 and for a rerun after an interruption at a commit; the cut at $N$; the stored files (frames of the states in order, lines byte-identical, options byte-identical); the records and their nulls per reason; the start of the modes (session start, overlay on frame 0); `accept` rejecting a clip; the rerun abort on a changed hash or $\gamma_{id}$; the bars and the last line on an abort.
