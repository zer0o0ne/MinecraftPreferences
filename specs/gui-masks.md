## Notation
- Shards: $N_S$ — the number of plan shards; plan shard $s = 0..N_S - 1$ — `{frames_dir}/plan/<name(s)>/shard.json`; label shard $s$ — `{labels_dir}/labels/<name(s)>/labels.json`; index shard $s$, features shard $s$ of a part — this block's, one per plan shard; $h$ — the complete shards of the set at hand; builds $b$ — `all`, then `fold0` … `fold<K-1>`; row block — image rows $[r_0, r_1)$, $r_0$ a multiple of `chunk_rows`, $r_1 = \min(r_0 + $`chunk_rows`$, H)$; position order — the frames of all plan shards concatenated in shard order.
- Frames $F$: the `ok` frames in position order with their label records (same `id`); $x_f \in \{0..255\}^{H \times W \times 3}$ the RGB view ($W, H$ from `{frames_dir}/run.json`); $y_f$ its `label`; $e_f$ its episode (`relpath` of its plan record); $g_f$ = its `gui_inventory`.
- $\mathcal{K}$ — catalogue classes in catalogue order; `other` — the reject label. Frames with $y_f \in$ {`invalid`, `no_answer`} are left out of building and evaluation, not of the split.
- $d_T(f, p) = \max_{ch} |x_f(p, ch) - T(p, ch)|$, computed in a signed integer type.
- Split: episodes in order of first appearance among the `ok` frames in position order; each gets one draw `rng.choice(3, p=split)` of `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64(seed))`: 0 = train, 1 = validation, 2 = test. $F^{tr}, F^{va}, F^{te}$ — labelled frames of each part; $F^{tr}_k = \{ f \in F^{tr} : y_f = k \}$.
- Qualified classes $\mathcal{Q}$: $k \in \mathcal{K}$ whose $F^{tr}_k$ spans ≥ `min_episodes` distinct episodes.
- Variant $V \in \{\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}, \mathrm{c}, \mathrm{d}, \mathrm{e}, \mathrm{f}\}$, in this order, written (a)–(f) in the text; for class $k$ a list of (template $T$, mask $M$) pairs — one per member in (a), (d), (e), (f), one in (b), (c).
- $\Gamma_m$ — the canvas of member $m$ in (1) (RGBA, $H \times W$); $\bar\Gamma_m$ — its RGB with pixels of alpha < 255 black.
- $d^V_T(f, p)$ — the deviation of variant $V$: $d_T(f, p)$ for $V \ne \mathrm{f}$, (12) for $V = \mathrm{f}$.
- $c^V_k(f)$, $|M|$ — the count of matching mask pixels and the mask size of the best pair (below), not reduced; $S^V_k(f) = c / |M|$ as an exact fraction.
- Text (9): $\Phi$ — the alpha channel of `font/ascii.png` under the textures directory; the cell of code point $c$: rows $8\lfloor c/16 \rfloor + [0, 8)$, columns $8(c \bmod 16) + [0, 8)$; $w(c)$ — glyph width; $\text{adv}(c)$ — advance; $u_i$ — pen position of the $i$-th character; $\omega$ — scale; $\chi$ — the label's colour (gui-screens `Label.color`, channels $\chi_R, \chi_G, \chi_B$); $G_o$ — pixels of the glyphs drawn at offset $o \in \{0, 1\}$.
- Rings (11): $R_k$ — the union of the 1-px frames of the slot hit rectangles of $k$.
- Edges (12): $L(x)$ — integer luma of an RGB image $x$; $E(x)$ — its edge magnitude.
- Folds (13): $K$ = `folds`; $e_0 \dots e_{n-1}$ — the training episodes (split draw 0, all of them, with or without labelled frames) in order of first appearance, as in the split; $\pi$ — their permutation; folds $l = 0..K-1$; $F^{tr}_{(l)}$ — training frames of fold-$l$ episodes; $F^{tr}_{(-l)} = F^{tr} \setminus F^{tr}_{(l)}$.
- Features (14): columns $i = 1..N_C$; $\varphi_{f,i}$ — feature $i$ of frame $f$; $q_\varphi$ — its quantile level.
- Config `configs/gui-masks.yaml` → `@dataclass(frozen=True) class GuiMasksConfig`: `frames_dir`, `labels_dir`, `screens` (path of `configs/gui-screens.yaml`), `split` (three decimals summing to 1), `seed`, `min_episodes` (integer ≥ 1), `spread_quantile` ($q$), `spread_max` ($s_{max}$), `patch` ($P$, divides 40), `patches_per_class` ($n_P$, ≥ 1, checked at load; ≤ number of patches, checked when $W, H$ are read at the start of the run), `eps` (> 0), `tau_grid` (integers), `theta_grid`, `mu_grid` (decimal strings, read as exact fractions), `chunk_rows` (even, ≥ 2; image rows read and processed at once in (2)), `folds` ($K$, integer ≥ 2), `fold_seed` (integer), `feature_quantile` ($q_\varphi$, $0 < q_\varphi \le 1$), `out_dir`. Violated constraints → `ValueError` at load.

## Math
(1) Variant (a), per member $m$ of $k$ ([U]: outline only): $\Gamma_m$ = the member's blits composited in draw order (each texture converted to RGBA, source-over) onto a transparent $H \times W$ canvas; $T = \bar\Gamma_m$; $M$ = pixels with canvas alpha 255, inside $k$'s window or `book_panel`, outside every element hit rectangle, every slot-content rectangle $(x_s, y_s, 17, 18)$, and every dynamic and label rectangle of any member of $k$. Members with an empty $M$ are skipped; a class with no pair left has no template.
(2) Variant (b), for $k \in \mathcal{Q}$ ([U]: low spread; quantile chosen 2026-10-10): $T(p, ch)$ = `quantile` over $F^{tr}_k$ of $x_f(p, ch)$ at 0.5, `inverted_cdf` (the lower median, `uint8`) [N]; $\sigma_k(p)$ = `quantile` over $F^{tr}_k$ of $d_T(f, p)$ at $q$, `inverted_cdf`; $M = \{ p : \sigma_k(p) \le s_{max} \}$; an empty $M$ leaves $k$ without a template in (b) and (c). Computed over row blocks of `chunk_rows` rows, the frames of $F^{tr}_k$ read one row block at a time: rows $[r_0, r_1)$ of the RGB view (5) from the Y rows $[r_0, r_1)$ and the U, V rows $[r_0/2, r_1/2)$ of `<id>.yuv`; every build of $k$ ((13): all of $F^{tr}$ and each fold) from the same block.
(3) Variant (c), built only when at least two classes have a (b) template, for those classes: patches $Q$ = the non-overlapping $P \times P$ squares tiling the frame from (0, 0); $\bar\sigma_k(Q)$ = mean of $\sigma_k$ over $Q$; $\delta_k(Q) = \min_{j \in \mathcal{Q}, j \ne k}$ mean over $Q$ of $\max_{ch} |T_k - T_j|$; $\rho_k(Q) = \delta_k(Q) / (\bar\sigma_k(Q) + \varepsilon)$ in float64; $M$ = union of the $n_P$ patches with the largest $\rho_k$ (ties: smaller top row, then smaller left column); $T$ = (b)'s template. Only classes with a (b) template take part, in $\delta_k$ too.
(4) Score: for each pair $(T, M)$ of $k$ in $V$, $c = |\{ p \in M : d^V_T(f, p) \le \tau \}|$; the pair with the largest $c / |M|$ (exact) is the best pair (ties: the first in its class's list); $S^V_k(f) = c / |M|$. Classes without a template have no score.
(5) Prediction: if no class has a score, $\hat y_f$ = `other`; else $k^*$ = the class with the largest $S_k$ (ties: catalogue order), $S_{(2)}$ = the second-largest score (0 if only one); $\hat y_f = k^*$ if $S_{k^*} \ge \theta$ and $S_{k^*} - S_{(2)} \ge \mu$ (exact fractions), else `other`.
(6) Thresholds per variant, two choices, each the grid point of `tau_grid` × `theta_grid` × `mu_grid` (τ outermost, μ innermost) maximising a criterion on $F^{va}$ (ties: the first in that order): `agreement` $= \frac{1}{|F^{va}|}\sum_f \mathbb{1}[\hat y_f = y_f]$; `macro_recall` = mean, over the labels present in $F^{va}$, of the share of their frames predicted correctly.
(7) Evaluation on $F^{te}$, per variant and threshold choice: agreement and macro recall; per label $k \in \mathcal{K} \cup$ {`other`}: count with $y_f = k$, count with $\hat y_f = k$, precision, recall, F1 $= 2TP / (n_{true} + n_{pred})$ (each `null` when its denominator is 0), the ids of frames with $y_f = k$ and $\hat y_f \ne k$; agreement and macro recall `null` on an empty set; the confusion matrix ($y$ × $\hat y$); the same split by $g_f$ true / false.
(8) Agreement between labellers, per threshold choice: labellers VLM, (a), (b), (c), (d), (e), (f); for each $k \in \mathcal{K}$: $F_k$ = frames of $F^{te}$ to which at least one labeller assigns $k$; $A_k[i, j] = |\{ f \in F_k : L_i(f) = L_j(f) \}| / |F_k|$, reported with $|F_k|$ (no matrix when $F_k$ is empty).
(9) Text of a label with text $s_1 \dots s_n$ (its `name`), rectangle $(x_0, y_0, w_0, h_0)$ and colour $\chi$ [G], [J], [R]: $\omega = 2$ if $h_0 = 18$, else 1; shadow iff $h_0 \in \{9, 18\}$; $w(c) = 1 + \max \{ i \in [0, 8) : \Phi \ne 0$ somewhere in column $i$ of $c$'s cell $\}$, 0 for an empty cell; $\text{adv}(c) = 4$ for the space, $w(c) + 1$ otherwise; $u_i = \sum_{j < i} \text{adv}(s_j)$; $G_o = \{ (x_0 + \omega(u_i + g_x + o) + a,\ y_0 + \omega(g_y + o) + b) : s_i \ne$ space, $\Phi \ne 0$ at offset $(g_x, g_y)$ of $s_i$'s cell, $g_x, g_y \in [0, 8)$, $a, b \in [0, \omega) \}$. Drawn on an RGB image: with a shadow, the pixels of $G_1$ take $\lfloor \chi / 4 \rfloor$ per channel, then the pixels of $G_0$ take $\chi$; the text pixels are $G_0 \cup G_1$ with a shadow, $G_0$ without.
(10) Variant (d), per member $m$ of $k$ ([U]: the whole label rectangle): $T = \bar\Gamma_m$ with every label of $m$ drawn by (9), in catalogue order; $M = \bigcup_\ell \big( ((\ell\text{'s rectangle} \cap \{ \Gamma_m \text{ alpha} = 255 \}) \setminus D_m) \cup \text{text pixels of } \ell \big)$ over the labels $\ell$ of $m$, $D_m$ = the union of $m$'s dynamic rectangles; where the canvas is transparent (full-screen classes, label parts outside the blits) the background is the world, so only the text pixels are in $M$. Members without labels are skipped.
(11) Variant (e), per member $m$ of $k$ ([U]: slot frames): $R_k$ = the union, over the elements of $k$ of kind `slot`, of the hit rectangle $(x, y, 18, 18)$ minus its interior $(x + 1, y + 1, 16, 16)$ (the 1-px frame, 68 pixels); $T = \bar\Gamma_m$; $M$ = pixels of $R_k$ with $\Gamma_m$ alpha 255, outside every dynamic and label rectangle of any member of $k$. Members with an empty $M$ are skipped.
(12) Variant (f) ([U]: edges): for an RGB image $x$, $L(x)(p) = 299 R(p) + 587 G(p) + 114 B(p)$ (integer, [BT601] × 1000) and $E(x)(p) = \max(|L(x)(p + (1, 0)) - L(x)(p)|, |L(x)(p + (0, 1)) - L(x)(p)|)$ for $p_x \le W - 2$, $p_y \le H - 2$; per member $m$ of $k$: $T = E(\bar\Gamma_m)$; $M = \{ p : p,\ p + (1, 0),\ p + (0, 1) \in M^a_m \cup M^e_m \}$, with $M^a_m$, $M^e_m$ the masks of $m$ in (1), (11) (empty where $m$ was skipped); $d^{\mathrm{f}}_T(f, p) = |E(x_f)(p) - T(p)| / 1000$ (exact). Members with an empty $M$ are skipped.
(13) Folds ([U]: cross-fitting): $\pi$ = `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64(fold_seed)).permutation(n)`; episode $e_{\pi[r]}$ gets fold $r \bmod K$, $r = 0..n-1$. Fold $l$ is built when $F^{tr}_{(l)}$ is not empty: (2) for each $k \in \mathcal{Q}$ over the frames of $F^{tr}_{(-l)}$ labelled $k$, when there is at least one (an empty $M$ leaves $k$ without a fold-$l$ template); (3), when the (c) of all of $F^{tr}$ is built, over the classes with a (b) template both from all of $F^{tr}$ and from fold $l$, when there are at least two. $\mathcal{Q}$ stays that of Notation, from all of $F^{tr}$.
(14) Features: one column $i$ per pair of (1)–(3), (10)–(12) built on all of $F^{tr}$ — variants in order, then classes in catalogue order, then members in catalogue order; for frame $f$ and the column of pair $(T, M)$ of class $k$ in $V$: $\varphi_{f,i}$ = `quantile` at $q_\varphi$, `inverted_cdf` [N], of the $|M|$ values $d^V_T(f, p)$, $p \in M$ (repeats kept), as float64. For $f \in F^{tr}_{(l)}$, a column of (b) or (c) uses $k$'s fold-$l$ pair of that variant instead, and is NaN when there is none. $F^{va}$, $F^{te}$ use the column's own pair.

## Behaviour
A run of `main`, in this order. Each stored set is a dataset of `specs/shards.md` (Datasets below), continued after its complete shards on a rerun. Memory holds the shards in work and the state: the split, the folds, $\mathcal{Q}$, the training frames (ids, folds) of each class of $\mathcal{Q}$, the counts, the accumulators of (6)–(8), one row block of a class's training frames in (2), the templates and spreads of one build in (3), and, from the start of (S), every template pair (the model).
(R) Checks, in this order, each failing one aborting with a `RuntimeError` (paths in messages as written in the config): `{frames_dir}/run.json` holds its counts, by `finished_run` of `specs/runs.md` with marker `frames` (`{frames_dir} is not complete`, also when the file is absent); `{labels_dir}` has $N_S$ complete label shards (`{labels_dir} has <n> label shards, not <N_S>`); the `frames` of `{labels_dir}/run.json` equals this run's `frames` below (`{labels_dir} does not label the current {frames_dir}`); `patches_per_class` ≤ the number of patches for $W$, $H$ (`patches_per_class <n> exceeds the <m> patches of a <W>x<H> frame`); if `{out_dir}/run.json` exists, its fingerprints equal this run's, compared in the order `config`, `screens`, `frames`, `labels` (`<name> changed since the run started; delete {out_dir}` for the first differing one): `sha256` of the config file and of the catalogue file (lowercase hex); `frames` = {`shards`: $N_S$, `ok`, `failed` of `{frames_dir}/run.json`, 0 when absent}; `labels` = the SHA-256 (lowercase hex) of the bytes of the label shards' `labels.json` files concatenated in shard order. Then `{out_dir}/run.json` = {`config` (the config file's mapping as written), `sha256`: {`config`, `screens`}, `frames`, `labels`} is written by `specs/shards.md` (4); at the end of a run that exits 0 it is rewritten the same way with `"complete": true` added: the outputs are complete then.
(I) Index: for $s = h..N_S - 1$, plan shard $s$ and label shard $s$ are read and index shard $s$ is written: its `ok` frames in position order, each with `id`, `episode` ($e_f$), `label` ($y_f$, `invalid` and `no_answer` included), `g` ($g_f$), `part` (the split draw of $e_f$, drawn when $e_f$ first appears); a `label` outside $\mathcal{K} \cup$ {`other`, `invalid`, `no_answer`} aborts with a `RuntimeError` (`label <label> of <id> is not a class of the catalogue`); a frame is `ok` when its `.yuv` exists, joined to its label record by `id` (after (R) the same set as the label records); `rng` — the split generator's `bit_generator.state` after the shard. Continuation: the parts of the episodes of the complete index shards are rebuilt from their frames and the generator set to the state of shard $h - 1$. Then, reading the index shards one at a time, the state: part counts, also per index shard, $\mathcal{Q}$, the training episodes in order of first appearance, the folds (13), the training frames of each class of $\mathcal{Q}$; an empty $F^{va}$ aborts here with a `RuntimeError`, before any template (`the validation part is empty`).
(T) Templates, each set continued after its complete shards: catalogue shard $s$ — class $s$ of $\mathcal{K}$: its pairs of (a), (d), (e), (f) ((1), (10)–(12)); then (b) shard $s$ — class $s$ of $\mathcal{Q}$ (catalogue order): the (2) pairs of its builds (`all` and each built fold of (13)), its training frames read one row block at a time by `rgb_rows`; then (c) shard $s$ — build $s$ in build order, always `folds` + 1 shards: the (3) masks of the classes taking part (none when (3) is not built for it), from the templates and spreads of that build read from the (b) shards one at a time.
(S) Scoring, frame-outer, each frame read once with the model in memory, for the parts in the order train, validation, test; features shard $s$ of a part holds the frames of $F^{tr}$, $F^{va}$ or $F^{te}$ in index shard $s$ (possibly none), in position order: train — (14); validation — (4) at every $\tau$ of `tau_grid` for every variant and class, and (14); then (6) from the validation shards read one at a time, its result written to `{out_dir}/thresholds.json` by `specs/shards.md` (4) and read from it by later runs; test — (4) at the chosen $\tau$ of each variant and threshold choice, (5), and (14).
(E) Report: (7), (8) accumulated over the test shards read one at a time; the split from the state, `pairs` from the model; `report.json` and `report.md` written with a plain write at the end of every run that exits 0.
