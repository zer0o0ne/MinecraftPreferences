## Notation
- Frames $F$: records of `{frames_dir}/frames.jsonl` with status `ok`, joined by `id` with `{labels_dir}/labels.jsonl`; $x_f \in \{0..255\}^{H \times W \times 3}$ the RGB view ($W, H$ from `{frames_dir}/run.json`); $y_f$ its label; $e_f$ its episode (`relpath`); $g_f$ = `line.isGuiInventory`.
- $\mathcal{K}$ — catalogue classes in catalogue order; `other` — the reject label. Frames with $y_f \in$ {`invalid`, `no_answer`} are left out of building and evaluation, not of the split.
- $d_T(f, p) = \max_{ch} |x_f(p, ch) - T(p, ch)|$, computed in a signed integer type.
- Split: episodes in order of first appearance in `frames.jsonl` (all `ok` records); each gets one draw `rng.choice(3, p=split)` of `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64(seed))`: 0 = train, 1 = validation, 2 = test. $F^{tr}, F^{va}, F^{te}$ — labelled frames of each part; $F^{tr}_k = \{ f \in F^{tr} : y_f = k \}$.
- Qualified classes $\mathcal{Q}$: $k \in \mathcal{K}$ whose $F^{tr}_k$ spans ≥ `min_episodes` distinct episodes.
- Variant $V \in \{\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}, \mathrm{c}, \mathrm{d}, \mathrm{e}, \mathrm{f}\}$, in this order, written (a)–(f) in the text; for class $k$ a list of (template $T$, mask $M$) pairs — one per member in (a), (d), (e), (f), one in (b), (c).
- $\Gamma_m$ — the canvas of member $m$ in (1) (RGBA, $H \times W$); $\bar\Gamma_m$ — its RGB with pixels of alpha < 255 black.
- $d^V_T(f, p)$ — the deviation of variant $V$: $d_T(f, p)$ for $V \ne \mathrm{f}$, (12) for $V = \mathrm{f}$.
- $c^V_k(f)$, $|M|$ — the count of matching mask pixels and the mask size of the best pair (below); $S^V_k(f) = c / |M|$ as an exact fraction.
- Text (9): $\Phi$ — the alpha channel of `font/ascii.png` under the textures directory; the cell of code point $c$: rows $8\lfloor c/16 \rfloor + [0, 8)$, columns $8(c \bmod 16) + [0, 8)$; $w(c)$ — glyph width; $\text{adv}(c)$ — advance; $u_i$ — pen position of the $i$-th character; $\omega$ — scale; $\chi$ — the label's colour (gui-screens `Label.color`, channels $\chi_R, \chi_G, \chi_B$); $G_o$ — pixels of the glyphs drawn at offset $o \in \{0, 1\}$.
- Rings (11): $R_k$ — the union of the 1-px frames of the slot hit rectangles of $k$.
- Edges (12): $L(x)$ — integer luma of an RGB image $x$; $E(x)$ — its edge magnitude.
- Folds (13): $K$ = `folds`; $e_0 \dots e_{n-1}$ — the training episodes (split draw 0, all of them, with or without labelled frames) in order of first appearance in `frames.jsonl` (all `ok` records, as in the split); $\pi$ — their permutation; folds $l = 0..K-1$; $F^{tr}_{(l)}$ — training frames of fold-$l$ episodes; $F^{tr}_{(-l)} = F^{tr} \setminus F^{tr}_{(l)}$.
- Features (14): columns $i = 1..N_C$; $\varphi_{f,i}$ — feature $i$ of frame $f$; $q_\varphi$ — its quantile level.
- Config `configs/gui-masks.yaml` → `@dataclass(frozen=True) class GuiMasksConfig`: `frames_dir`, `labels_dir`, `screens` (path of `configs/gui-screens.yaml`), `split` (three decimals summing to 1), `seed`, `min_episodes` (integer ≥ 1), `spread_quantile` ($q$), `spread_max` ($s_{max}$), `patch` ($P$, divides 40), `patches_per_class` ($n_P$, ≥ 1, checked at load; ≤ number of patches, checked when $W, H$ are read at the start of the run), `eps` (> 0), `tau_grid` (integers), `theta_grid`, `mu_grid` (decimal strings, read as exact fractions), `chunk_rows` (image rows processed at once in (2)), `folds` ($K$, integer ≥ 2), `fold_seed` (integer), `feature_quantile` ($q_\varphi$, $0 < q_\varphi \le 1$), `out_dir`. Violated constraints → `ValueError` at load.

## Math
(1) Variant (a), per member $m$ of $k$ ([U]: outline only): $\Gamma_m$ = the member's blits composited in draw order (each texture converted to RGBA, source-over) onto a transparent $H \times W$ canvas; $T = \bar\Gamma_m$; $M$ = pixels with canvas alpha 255, inside $k$'s window or `book_panel`, outside every element hit rectangle, every slot-content rectangle $(x_s, y_s, 17, 18)$, and every dynamic and label rectangle of any member of $k$. Members with an empty $M$ are skipped; a class with no pair left has no template.
(2) Variant (b), for $k \in \mathcal{Q}$ ([U]: low spread; quantile chosen 2026-10-10): $T(p, ch)$ = `quantile` over $F^{tr}_k$ of $x_f(p, ch)$ at 0.5, `inverted_cdf` (the lower median, `uint8`) [N]; $\sigma_k(p)$ = `quantile` over $F^{tr}_k$ of $d_T(f, p)$ at $q$, `inverted_cdf`; $M = \{ p : \sigma_k(p) \le s_{max} \}$; an empty $M$ leaves $k$ without a template in (b) and (c). Computed over row blocks of `chunk_rows` rows.
(3) Variant (c), built only when at least two classes have a (b) template, for those classes: patches $Q$ = the non-overlapping $P \times P$ squares tiling the frame from (0, 0); $\bar\sigma_k(Q)$ = mean of $\sigma_k$ over $Q$; $\delta_k(Q) = \min_{j \in \mathcal{Q}, j \ne k}$ mean over $Q$ of $\max_{ch} |T_k - T_j|$; $\rho_k(Q) = \delta_k(Q) / (\bar\sigma_k(Q) + \varepsilon)$ in float64; $M$ = union of the $n_P$ patches with the largest $\rho_k$ (ties: smaller top row, then smaller left column); $T$ = (b)'s template. Only classes with a (b) template take part, in $\delta_k$ too.
(4) Score: for each pair $(T, M)$ of $k$ in $V$, $c = |\{ p \in M : d^V_T(f, p) \le \tau \}|$; the pair with the largest $c / |M|$ (exact) is the best pair; $S^V_k(f) = c / |M|$. Classes without a template have no score.
(5) Prediction: if no class has a score, $\hat y_f$ = `other`; else $k^*$ = the class with the largest $S_k$ (ties: catalogue order), $S_{(2)}$ = the second-largest score (0 if only one); $\hat y_f = k^*$ if $S_{k^*} \ge \theta$ and $S_{k^*} - S_{(2)} \ge \mu$ (exact fractions), else `other`.
(6) Thresholds per variant, two choices, each the grid point of `tau_grid` × `theta_grid` × `mu_grid` (τ outermost, μ innermost) maximising a criterion on $F^{va}$ (ties: the first in that order): `agreement` $= \frac{1}{|F^{va}|}\sum_f \mathbb{1}[\hat y_f = y_f]$; `macro_recall` = mean, over the labels present in $F^{va}$, of the share of their frames predicted correctly.
(7) Evaluation on $F^{te}$, per variant and threshold choice: agreement and macro recall; per label $k \in \mathcal{K} \cup$ {`other`}: count with $y_f = k$, count with $\hat y_f = k$, precision, recall, F1 $= 2TP / (n_{true} + n_{pred})$ (each `null` when its denominator is 0), the ids of frames with $y_f = k$ and $\hat y_f \ne k$; agreement and macro recall `null` on an empty set; the confusion matrix ($y$ × $\hat y$); the same split by $g_f$ true / false.
(8) Agreement between labellers, per threshold choice: labellers VLM, (a), (b), (c), (d), (e), (f); for each $k \in \mathcal{K}$: $F_k$ = frames of $F^{te}$ to which at least one labeller assigns $k$; $A_k[i, j] = |\{ f \in F_k : L_i(f) = L_j(f) \}| / |F_k|$, reported with $|F_k|$ (no matrix when $F_k$ is empty).
(9) Text of a label with text $s_1 \dots s_n$ (its `name`), rectangle $(x_0, y_0, w_0, h_0)$ and colour $\chi$ [G], [J], [R]: $\omega = 2$ if $h_0 = 18$, else 1; shadow iff $h_0 \in \{9, 18\}$; $w(c) = 1 + \max \{ i \in [0, 8) : \Phi \ne 0$ somewhere in column $i$ of $c$'s cell $\}$, 0 for an empty cell; $\text{adv}(c) = 4$ for the space, $w(c) + 1$ otherwise; $u_i = \sum_{j < i} \text{adv}(s_j)$; $G_o = \{ (x_0 + \omega(u_i + g_x + o) + a,\ y_0 + \omega(g_y + o) + b) : s_i \ne$ space, $\Phi \ne 0$ at offset $(g_x, g_y)$ of $s_i$'s cell, $g_x, g_y \in [0, 8)$, $a, b \in [0, \omega) \}$. Drawn on an RGB image: with a shadow, the pixels of $G_1$ take $\lfloor \chi / 4 \rfloor$ per channel, then the pixels of $G_0$ take $\chi$; the text pixels are $G_0 \cup G_1$ with a shadow, $G_0$ without.
(10) Variant (d), per member $m$ of $k$ ([U]: the whole label rectangle): $T = \bar\Gamma_m$ with every label of $m$ drawn by (9), in catalogue order; $M = \bigcup_\ell \big( ((\ell\text{'s rectangle} \cap \{ \Gamma_m \text{ alpha} = 255 \}) \setminus D_m) \cup \text{text pixels of } \ell \big)$ over the labels $\ell$ of $m$, $D_m$ = the union of $m$'s dynamic rectangles; where the canvas is transparent (full-screen classes, label parts outside the blits) the background is the world, so only the text pixels are in $M$. Members without labels are skipped.
(11) Variant (e), per member $m$ of $k$ ([U]: slot frames): $R_k$ = the union, over the elements of $k$ of kind `slot`, of the hit rectangle $(x, y, 18, 18)$ minus its interior $(x + 1, y + 1, 16, 16)$ (the 1-px frame, 68 pixels); $T = \bar\Gamma_m$; $M$ = pixels of $R_k$ with $\Gamma_m$ alpha 255, outside every dynamic and label rectangle of any member of $k$. Members with an empty $M$ are skipped.
(12) Variant (f) ([U]: edges): for an RGB image $x$, $L(x)(p) = 299 R(p) + 587 G(p) + 114 B(p)$ (integer, [BT601] × 1000) and $E(x)(p) = \max(|L(x)(p + (1, 0)) - L(x)(p)|, |L(x)(p + (0, 1)) - L(x)(p)|)$ for $p_x \le W - 2$, $p_y \le H - 2$; per member $m$ of $k$: $T = E(\bar\Gamma_m)$; $M = \{ p : p,\ p + (1, 0),\ p + (0, 1) \in M^a_m \cup M^e_m \}$, with $M^a_m$, $M^e_m$ the masks of $m$ in (1), (11) (empty where $m$ was skipped); $d^{\mathrm{f}}_T(f, p) = |E(x_f)(p) - T(p)| / 1000$ (exact). Members with an empty $M$ are skipped.
(13) Folds ([U]: cross-fitting): $\pi$ = `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64(fold_seed)).permutation(n)`; episode $e_{\pi[r]}$ gets fold $r \bmod K$, $r = 0..n-1$. Fold $l$ is built when $F^{tr}_{(l)}$ is not empty: (2) for each $k \in \mathcal{Q}$ over the frames of $F^{tr}_{(-l)}$ labelled $k$, when there is at least one (an empty $M$ leaves $k$ without a fold-$l$ template); (3), when the (c) of all of $F^{tr}$ is built, over the classes with a (b) template both from all of $F^{tr}$ and from fold $l$, when there are at least two. $\mathcal{Q}$ stays that of Notation, from all of $F^{tr}$.
(14) Features: one column $i$ per pair of (1)–(3), (10)–(12) built on all of $F^{tr}$ — variants in order, then classes in catalogue order, then members in catalogue order; for frame $f$ and the column of pair $(T, M)$ of class $k$ in $V$: $\varphi_{f,i}$ = `quantile` at $q_\varphi$, `inverted_cdf` [N], of the $|M|$ values $d^V_T(f, p)$, $p \in M$ (repeats kept), as float64. For $f \in F^{tr}_{(l)}$, a column of (b) or (c) uses $k$'s fold-$l$ pair of that variant instead, and is NaN when there is none. $F^{va}$, $F^{te}$ use the column's own pair.

## Interface
Code of this block lives in the subpackage `src/mcpref/recognition/` (moved from `src/mcpref/gui/`, which keeps the catalogue, textures and labels: `screens`, `textures`, `labels`, `label_files`); tests mirror it in `tests/recognition/`.
- `recognition/masks.py` — (1)–(3):
  - `def texture_pairs(cls: ScreenClass, textures_dir: Path, width: int, height: int) -> list[tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]]` — (1).
  - `def variance_pair(frames: Sequence[np.ndarray], q: float, s_max: int, chunk_rows: int, step: Callable[[], None]) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray, np.ndarray]` — (2): template (`uint8`), spread, mask (`bool`); calls `step()` once after each row block (bar `templates b`).
  - `def patch_masks(templates: Mapping[str, np.ndarray], spreads: Mapping[str, np.ndarray], patch: int, n_p: int, eps: float, step: Callable[[], None]) -> dict[str, np.ndarray]` — (3); calls `step()` once after each class's mask (bar `templates c`).
- `recognition/drawn_masks.py` — (9)–(12), from the catalogue and textures only:
  - `def text_pixels(label: Label, font: np.ndarray, width: int, height: int) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]` — (9): $G_0$, $G_1$ (`bool`, $H \times W$; $G_1$ empty without a shadow); `font` = $\Phi$.
  - `def label_pairs(cls: ScreenClass, textures_dir: Path, width: int, height: int) -> dict[str, tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]]` — (10), member → pair.
  - `def ring_pairs(cls: ScreenClass, textures_dir: Path, width: int, height: int) -> dict[str, tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]]` — (11), member → pair.
  - `def edges(rgb: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray` — (12): $E$, `int64`, $H \times W$, 0 in the last row and column (no mask contains them).
  - `def edge_pairs(cls: ScreenClass, textures_dir: Path, width: int, height: int) -> dict[str, tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]]` — (12), member → ($E(\bar\Gamma_m)$, $M$).
- `recognition/pairs.py` — the pairs of every variant on all of $F^{tr}$ and per fold, (1)–(3), (10)–(13); bars `templates catalogue`, `templates b`, `templates c`; `VARIANTS` — the variant names in order (`a` … `f`), also used by block gui-stack.
- `recognition/frame_set.py` — Notation $F$, the parts, $\mathcal{Q}$, and (13): `def folds(relpaths: Sequence[str], k: int, seed: int) -> dict[str, int]`, `relpaths` = $e_0 \dots e_{n-1}$.
- `recognition/recognise.py` — (4), (5), (14) and the loops over frames (bars `features train`, `score <part>`): `def scores(x: np.ndarray, pairs: Mapping[str, Sequence[tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]]], tau: int) -> dict[str, Fraction]`; `def predict(scores: Mapping[str, Fraction], order: Sequence[str], theta: Fraction, mu: Fraction) -> str`; `def pair_feature(x: np.ndarray, t: np.ndarray, m: np.ndarray, q: float) -> float` — (14) for one pair. For (f) both run on one-channel arrays: $x = E(x_f)$, $t = E(\bar\Gamma_m)$ with a trailing channel axis of size 1; the caller passes $1000\tau$ as `tau` to `scores` and divides the result of `pair_feature` by 1000; $d_T$ computed in `int64`. τ is reported in `tau_grid` units for every variant.
- `recognition/metrics.py` — (6)–(8), also used by block gui-stack; `CRITERIA` — mapping threshold choice → its criterion of (6), in the order `agreement`, `macro_recall`:
  - `def evaluation(y: Sequence[str], y_hat: Sequence[str], ids: Sequence[str], g: Sequence[bool], labels: Sequence[str]) -> dict` — (7) on the frames given, with `by_flag`.
  - `def matrices(y: Sequence[str], preds: Mapping[str, Mapping[str, Sequence[str]]], names: Sequence[str]) -> dict` — (8): `preds` maps labeller → threshold choice → $\hat y$ over the frames; labellers VLM, then `preds` in its order.

- `recognition/evaluate.py` — config, split, `main`: `GuiMasksConfig`; `def load_config(path: Path) -> GuiMasksConfig`; `def split_episodes(relpaths: Sequence[str], probs: Sequence[float], seed: int) -> dict[str, int]`; `def main(argv: Sequence[str]) -> int`, run as `python -m mcpref.recognition.evaluate <config path>`; exit 0, or 1 with a one-line message as the last line of stderr (also for an empty validation set, checked right after the split, before any template is built or written, or for a `patches_per_class` above the number of patches); progress bars on stderr (below; scoring takes ~1 s per frame on a CPU); the validation frames are scored and the thresholds chosen before the test frames are scored.

- Produces `{out_dir}/`:

  - `report.md`: the same as tables, titled `GUI masks report`;
  - `features.npz` for block gui-stack (`numpy.savez`, no object arrays): `columns` (str, $N_C$: `<variant>/<class>[__<member>]`), `labels` (str: $\mathcal{K}$ in catalogue order, then `other`); per part `train`, `validation`, `test` the frames of $F^{tr}$, $F^{va}$, $F^{te}$ in `frames.jsonl` order: `<part>_ids` (str), `<part>_y` (str, $y_f$), `<part>_g` (bool, $g_f$), `<part>_x` (float64, frames × $N_C$, (14); NaN only in (b), (c) columns of `train`); `test_pred_<variant>_<choice>` (str, $\hat y_f$ of (7)) for the six variants and both threshold choices.
- Script `scripts/eval_gui_masks.sh` (preamble of `specs/spark-env.md`): `uv run --locked python -m mcpref.recognition.evaluate configs/gui-masks.yaml`; block gui-stack appends its line in its own cycle. The VLM server need not run; stopping it frees its memory.
- Progress bars (AGENTS.md Code), in this order, nothing else printed per frame, no postfix:
  - `templates catalogue` (unit `class`): (1), (10)–(12) over the classes of $\mathcal{K}$; total $= |\mathcal{K}|$; advanced after each class's pairs of (a), (d), (e), (f) (~2.6 s in all on a CPU); $\mathcal{K}$ empty → the line `templates catalogue: nothing to do` instead.
  - `templates b` (unit `step`): (2) over $\mathcal{Q}$, on all of $F^{tr}$ and on each built fold of (13); each training frame loaded once for all builds of its class; total $= \sum_{k \in \mathcal{Q}} (|F^{tr}_k| + (1 + n_k) \lceil H / \text{chunk\_rows} \rceil)$, $n_k$ = the built folds $l$ with a frame of $F^{tr}_{(-l)}$ labelled $k$; advanced after each training frame is loaded and after each row block (`step`); $\mathcal{Q}$ empty → the line `templates b: nothing to do` instead.
  - `templates c` (unit `class`): (3) on all of $F^{tr}$ and on each fold where it is built; total = the classes taking part, summed over these builds; advanced after each class's mask (`step`); no build → the line `templates c: nothing to do` instead.
  - `features train` (unit `frame`): (14) over $F^{tr}$; total $= |F^{tr}|$; advanced after each frame's features; $F^{tr}$ empty → the line `features train: nothing to do` instead.
  - `score validation`, then `thresholds`, then `score test`: `score <part>` (unit `frame`): (4) and (14) for the six variants in turn; total = 6 × the part's frames; advanced after one frame's scores for every τ and its features, for one variant; no test frame → the line `score test: nothing to do` instead. `thresholds` (unit `point`): (6) for the six variants in turn; total = 6 × |`tau_grid`| × |`theta_grid`| × |`mu_grid`|; advanced after each grid point's predictions.

## Out of scope
- Rebuilding on engine frames (the same code later run on other frames and labels).
- Templates for `other`; recognising windows at other offsets or scales.
- Correcting VLM labels.
- Texts that vary with game state (dynamic rectangles of the catalogue); glyphs outside `ascii.png`; hovered or inactive button drawings in (d), (e).
- Cross-fitting (a), (d)–(f): they use no frames.
